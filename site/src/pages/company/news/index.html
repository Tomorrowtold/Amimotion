News. Awards, stages and press.

What AmIMotion has won, where it has presented, and who has written about it.

This page lists the awards AmIMotion has won, the stages it has presented on, and what the press has written about it, newest first. AmIMotion is a brand of Locatepro Technologies Pvt Ltd, Bengaluru, India.

This year.

15 September 2026

Winner of NXP's Startup Impact Showcase 2026.

AmIMotion was named winner of the Startup Impact Showcase 2026, run by NXP Semiconductors in India. The award carries a prize of ₹2,00,000 and was presented by Hitesh Garg, VP and India Managing Director of NXP Semiconductors.

19 June 2026

Winner of the NXP India Tech Startup Challenge, Season 6.

Locatepro Technologies, the company behind AmIMotion, was awarded the title of winner of the NXP India Tech Startup Challenge, Season 6. The certificate is signed by Hitesh Garg of NXP Semiconductors.

May 2026

On stage at the Maruti Suzuki Incubation Program.

AmIMotion presented its tags, anchors and AmIHub at the Cohort 5 grand finale of the Maruti Suzuki Incubation Program, a programme for mobility start-ups run by Maruti Suzuki Innovation.
