AmIMotion is a brand of Locatepro Technologies Pvt Ltd, Bengaluru, India. It makes AmIGo tags, AmIMo anchors and AmIHub software. AmIMotion builds industrial spatial intelligence. It gives a time-stamped location to every trolley, bin, tool and vehicle you tag in a plant. It joins that location with your zones and with the jobs you link to each carrier. Your team, your systems and AI assistants can then answer where work is and where it waits. It is the first layer of Physical AI for factories and warehouses. Designed and built in India on NXP Trimension UWB silicon. Built on the FiRa and omlox specifications.
