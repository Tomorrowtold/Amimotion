Designed in Bengaluru. Built in India.

AmIMotion makes the tags, the anchors and the software that show where work is in a plant, and where it waits. One team builds all three.

AmIMotion is spatial intelligence for factories and warehouses. It is a brand of Locatepro Technologies Pvt Ltd, Bengaluru, India. AmIMotion designs and builds AmIGo tags, AmIMo anchors and AmIHub software, on NXP Trimension UWB silicon. It is built on the FiRa and omlox specifications. AmIMotion is led by Kaushal Kukkar, founder and CEO, and Vivek Dham, CTO.

What we make.

  • AmIGo tag

    Rides on what moves. Sends a short UWB pulse when it moves and sleeps when it stops.

  • AmIMo anchor

    Hears every tag. Fixed to a column or wall, it needs only power.

  • AmIHub

    One live map. Runs on an industrial PC inside your plant, with no internet needed.

How we work.

  • One team, every layer

    AmIMotion designs the tags, the anchors and AmIHub. One contract and one support line cover every layer.

  • Made in India

    Designed and built in India, with local stock and local engineers on your site.

  • Open by design

    Built on the FiRa and omlox specifications. AmIHub connects through open REST, MQTT, WebSocket and omlox hub interfaces, so your team or your integrator can build on it.

  • One bay first

    Every AmIMotion pilot starts with one bay or line. Then it grows plant-wide on the same AmIHub, without re-fitting the first bay.

What we will not claim.

A position is only useful when you know what it can and cannot tell you.

A position shows where a trolley is, and AmIHub says when it was last seen. It does not show which job the trolley carries until that job is linked to it. It does not say why work waited. That is for your team to say.

This site names no customer and shows no customer result. AmIMotion is built on the FiRa and omlox specifications, and claims no formal certification.
