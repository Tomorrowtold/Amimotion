Built for real floors.

AmIMotion, a brand of Locatepro Technologies Pvt Ltd in Bengaluru, India, builds industrial spatial intelligence for factories and warehouses with a complete UWB real-time location system: AmIGo tags, AmIMo anchors and AmIHub software. Factories use it to see where work in progress is and where it waits; warehouses use it to time pallets, totes and forklifts from dock to stock. Designed and built in India on NXP Trimension UWB silicon. Built on the FiRa and omlox specifications.
