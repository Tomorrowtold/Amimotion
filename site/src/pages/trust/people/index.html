People and privacy.

What a pilot tags, and why.

The AmIMotion pilot tags trolleys, bins and pallets, not people. Tagging people would be a separate project. It would need its own policy. You also set how long the map keeps its history.

On the trolley, not the person.

A small tag rides on each trolley, bin and tool. It goes on the trolley, not on the person pushing it.

You set who sees what, by role. Why it waited is for your team to say.
