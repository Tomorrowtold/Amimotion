People and privacy. The tag goes on the trolley.

An AmIMotion pilot follows work, not workers. Here is what it tags, what the map shows and who decides.

An AmIMotion pilot tags trolleys, bins and pallets, not people. The AmIGo tag goes on the trolley, not on the person pushing it. AmIHub's map shows where tagged things are and how long they have waited. Tagging people would be a separate project. It would need its own policy. You set who sees what, by role, and how long the map keeps its history.

What the map shows.

AmIHub shows where each tagged trolley, bin or pallet is, and how long it has waited in a zone. It shows where a tag was last seen, and when.

In the AmIMotion pilot, no person carries a tag. So no person is on the map, and the map does not show who pushed a trolley.

Why it waited is for your team to say. A long wait shows where to look in the flow. It does not say who is to blame.

Who sees what.

  • Access by role

    You set who sees what, by role. Each system and each AI assistant gets its own access, and every request is logged. Trust and data.

  • History you set

    You also set how long the map keeps its history.

  • Inside your plant

    AmIHub can run on an industrial PC inside your plant, with no internet needed.

If you ever want to tag people.

It is never part of an AmIMotion WIP pilot. Tagging people would be a separate project, outside the pilot. It would need its own policy. Talk to us about it before anything is tagged. How a pilot runs.

In India, a location tied to a named person is personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Your own policy would need to cover it. Privacy.

Questions teams ask first.

Does AmIMotion track workers?

The AmIMotion pilot tags trolleys, bins and pallets, not people. The tag goes on the trolley, not on the person pushing it. Tagging people would be a separate project. It would need its own policy.

Can a supervisor see who moved a trolley?

No. The map shows where the trolley went and when. No person carries a tag in the pilot, so the map does not show who pushed it.

Who decides how long the history is kept?

You do. You set how long the map keeps its history.

Does a tag on a forklift track its driver?

Forklifts are not part of the WIP pilot. If you tag vehicles later, the map shows the vehicle, and your policy decides how that history is used.
