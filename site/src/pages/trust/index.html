Trust and data. Where your data lives.

AmIHub can run inside your plant with no internet needed. This page says who can read what, and what each claim on this site rests on.

AmIMotion's AmIHub runs on an industrial PC inside your plant, with no internet needed, or in the cloud. Every interface uses TLS. You set who sees what, by role. Where it runs.

  • Inside your plant

    AmIHub runs on an industrial PC inside your plant, with no internet needed. Positions, zones and history do not have to leave your network.

  • In the cloud

    AmIHub can also run in the cloud. The same access controls, audit logs and history settings apply in both.

Who can read it.

  • Encrypted in transit

    AmIHub uses TLS on every interface, from REST and MQTT to the MCP server.

  • Access by role

    You set who sees what, by role. Each system and each AI assistant gets its own API key and scope.

  • Every request logged

    AmIHub keeps an audit log. For an AI assistant, it records who asked, what was called and what changed.

  • History you set

    You also set how long the map keeps its history.

  • A person approves changes

    Asking changes nothing. Any change, such as raising a work order in AmIHub, needs a person's approval. You can require it from a supervisor. We show it live on a call. Ask your floor.

  • Firmware over the air

    AmIHub updates anchor and tag firmware over the air.

What the claims rest on.

Every figure on this site has a source and a condition.

  • Product figures

    They come from AmIMotion's own published specifications and are indicative. Down to ±10 cm needs AmIMo anchors and depends on your floor. A battery life of 1 to 3 years depends on use. Final datasheets are shared under NDA.

  • Standards

    AmIMotion is built on the FiRa and omlox specifications. It claims no formal certification.

  • What a position cannot tell you

    A position does not show which job a trolley carries until the job is linked to it. It does not show that work is done, or why it waited. Those need your links, your zones and your team.

  • Customers

    This site names no customer and shows no customer result.

Questions teams ask first.

Does AmIHub need the internet?

No. AmIHub runs on an industrial PC inside your plant, with no internet needed. It can also run in the cloud.

Can an AI assistant change anything in AmIHub?

Asking changes nothing. Each assistant gets its own access: read-only, or only the actions you allow. Any change, such as raising a work order, needs a person's approval, and you can require it from a supervisor. Every request it makes is logged. AmIMotion shows it live on a call.

Does AmIMotion hold a formal certification?

AmIMotion is built on the FiRa and omlox specifications. It claims no formal certification.

Does the pilot track people?

No. The AmIMotion pilot tags trolleys, bins and pallets, not people. Tagging people would be a separate project. It would need its own policy.
