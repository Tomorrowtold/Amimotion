Clear claims. Clear boundaries.

What each claim on this site rests on, and what your floor still has to show.

AmIMotion’s product figures, such as down to ±10 cm in 3D (with AmIMo anchors) and 1 to 3 years of tag battery (typical), come from its published brochure and are indicative. A pilot measures them on your floor.

  • Product facts

    From the brochure. Figures are indicative, not independently measured. Confirm hardware revision, software version and setup in your evaluation.

  • Pictures and films

    Made to explain. Pictures, films and scenes on this site explain the product. They are not customer sites or live data.

  • What location does not prove

    Its limits. Which job is on a trolley, that work is done, why something waited, or that an action is allowed. Those need agreed links, rules and permissions.

  • Standards

    Built on the specifications. AmIMotion is built on the FiRa and omlox specifications. This site does not claim FiRa or omlox certification.

  • Interfaces and hosting

    Confirmed per site. REST, MQTT, WebSocket and an omlox hub interface, on-premise or cloud. Connectors and responsibilities are confirmed per site.

  • Analytics and AI

    Shown in a demo. Analytics views and MCP assistants are shown in a live, scoped demo. This site claims no robot control or measured business gains.

Security and your data. Where it lives, who can read it.

The same controls apply on-premise and in the cloud.

  • On-premise

    AmIHub runs on an industrial PC on your network with no internet dependency. Positions, zones and history stay on your site.

  • Cloud

    A cloud tenant, with the hosting region and terms set in your contract.

  • TLS

    Every interface, from REST and MQTT to the MCP server, is encrypted.

  • Roles and API keys

    Roles per person, keys per system or AI assistant, each with its own scope.

  • Audit logs and retention

    Every call is logged. You set how long history is kept.

  • Over the air

    Anchor and tag firmware is updated from AmIHub. Release notes and the update process are in the detailed pack.

  • For developers Interfaces, events and the MCP server.
  • Specifications Every figure we publish, and where it comes from.
