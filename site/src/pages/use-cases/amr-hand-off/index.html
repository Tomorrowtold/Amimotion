The hand-off, on the map.

Tag the robot and the trolley. The map shows them move together, and where they arrive.

With AmIMotion, robots, forklifts, trolleys and people share one live map, each located down to ±10 cm in 3D. Each arrival gets a time and a place, with no scan.

Today, and with AmIMotion.

  • Today

    Robots move material, but the plant knows what a robot carried only if someone scanned it. A missed scan means a wrong record.

  • With AmIMotion

    The robot tows a tagged trolley from pick-up to the line. Both tags move together on the map, and the destination zone records the arrival time.

Common questions.

Do we need to integrate with the robot?

To locate it, the robot only needs a tag. Any deeper link with your fleet software is scoped per site.

Does AmIMotion dispatch the robot?

No. Your fleet software drives, routes and dispatches. AmIMotion shows where the robot and its load are.

How do you track forklifts and AMRs indoors to a few centimetres?

Fit each with a UWB tag. AmIMo anchors locate them down to ±10 cm in 3D, on the same map as trolleys and people.
