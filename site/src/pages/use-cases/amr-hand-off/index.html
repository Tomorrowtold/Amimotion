The robot brings a trolley. AmIHub records the arrival.

Tag the AMR and the trolley it tows. AmIMotion shows them move together, and where they arrive.

With AmIMotion, an AMR and the trolley it tows each carry an AmIGo tag. AmIMo anchors place both down to ±10 cm, depending on your floor. AmIHub shows them move together and records when they arrive in the drop-off zone. Nobody scans the trolley. Your fleet software still drives, routes and dispatches the robot.

Robotics and AMRs

Today, and with AmIMotion.

  • Today.

    Robots move material, but the plant knows what a robot brought only if someone scanned it. A missed scan means a wrong record.

  • With AmIMotion.

    The robot tows a tagged trolley from pick-up to the line. Both tags move together on AmIHub's map. The drop-off zone records the arrival time.

From pick-up to drop-off.

  1. Pick-up.

    The robot's tag and the trolley's tag enter the pick-up zone. AmIHub notes the time.

  2. Together.

    Both tags move along the aisle together, on one map.

  3. Drop-off.

    Both enter the drop-off zone. AmIHub records where and when they arrived.

  4. Wait.

    The robot leaves. The trolley waits at the line, and its wait shows live, during the shift.

AmIMotion watches. It does not drive.

AmIMotion adds a second record beside your fleet's: where each robot and its load went, and when.

  • AmIMotion keeps.

    Where the robot and its trolley are, when they entered and left each zone, and their trips over any time window. AmIHub sends each event to any system you name, as it happens.

  • Your fleet software keeps.

    Driving, routing, dispatch and safety. Any deeper link with it is scoped for your site.

Common questions.

Do we need to integrate with the robot?

To locate it, the robot only needs an AmIGo tag. Any deeper link with your fleet software is scoped for your site.

Does AmIMotion dispatch the robot?

No. Your fleet software drives, routes and dispatches. AmIMotion shows where the robot and its load are.

Can forklifts and tuggers hand off the same way?

Yes. A forklift or tugger with an AmIGo vehicle tag shows on the same AmIHub map, and its arrivals are timed the same way.

Does AmIHub know which job the trolley carries?

Only through a link. You link the trolley to its job in AmIHub, or from your own system. The robot's tag says where, not what.
