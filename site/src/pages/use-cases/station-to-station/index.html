Every station move, timed.

Each station is a zone. When a tagged trolley enters or leaves, AmIHub records the time.

With AmIMotion, each tagged trolley is located down to ±10 cm in 3D and timed at every station zone. AmIHub can send each zone event to your ERP or MES under rules your plant agrees.

Today, and with AmIMotion.

  • Today

    Work status lives in the ERP, but it changes only when someone scans or types. Between scans the order is out of sight.

  • With AmIMotion

    Kitting, Machining, Assembly and Dispatch are zones on the map. Each time the trolley enters or leaves one, AmIHub records the time and can send the event on.

Common questions.

Which ERP does it work with?

AmIHub sends events over REST, MQTT, WebSocket and the omlox hub interface. The connector for your ERP is confirmed during scoping.

Does entering a zone mean the step is done?

Only if your plant says so. What counts as done is a rule you set, such as “entering Assembly ends Machining”.

What if a trolley sits too long?

Time in each zone is recorded, so long waits are easy to see. Who on your team acts when work waits too long is agreed before the pilot.
