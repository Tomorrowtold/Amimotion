Each station is a zone. Each move gets a time.

Your ERP changes a job's status when someone scans or types. AmIHub times the trolley at every station in between.

With AmIMotion, each station is a zone you name in AmIHub: kitting, machining, assembly, dispatch. An AmIGo tag rides on each trolley, and AmIMo anchors place it down to ±10 cm, depending on your floor. AmIHub notes when the trolley enters and leaves each zone, and sends each entry and exit to your MES or ERP as it happens.

Today, and with AmIMotion.

  • Today.

    Status lives in the ERP. It changes only when someone scans at a desk or types. Between scans, the order is out of sight.

  • With AmIMotion.

    Kitting, machining, assembly and dispatch are zones in AmIHub. Each time the trolley enters or leaves one, AmIHub records the time and sends the event on.

Entering a zone is not finishing a step.

AmIHub records where a trolley was and when. Whether a step is done comes from your MES or your people.

  • What AmIHub records.

    Each zone entry and exit, its time, and how long the trolley stayed.

  • What your MES records.

    The job, its route and its status. AmIMotion adds the times in between.

  • What you agree first.

    Which zones count, how to count a wait that runs past shift end, and who acts when work waits too long.

Common questions.

Which ERP or MES does it work with?

AmIHub sends events over REST, MQTT, WebSocket and the omlox hub interface. The connector for your system is confirmed when we scope the pilot.

Does entering a zone mean the step is done?

No. AmIHub records a time and a place. Whether a step is done comes from your MES or your people.

What if a trolley sits too long?

AmIHub records the time in each zone, so a long wait shows while it is still waiting. Who on your team acts when work waits too long is agreed before the pilot.

How is dwell time measured in manufacturing?

With AmIMotion, from the moment a tagged trolley enters a zone to the moment it leaves. AmIHub keeps both times, so every wait has a start and a length. How waits are timed.

UWB or RFID for this?

An RFID read records a pass at a gate. With AmIMotion, a UWB tag is placed as it moves, so AmIHub sees the time between gates too. Compare the two on one workflow of yours. UWB vs RFID.
