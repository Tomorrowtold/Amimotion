What can you do with an RTLS in a factory?

With AmIMotion, four things. Find tagged trolleys and tools from a screen. Time work at every station. See what robots brought, and when. Ask an AI assistant about the floor, answered from live data in AmIHub.

Which use case should we start with?

The one where people walk most to find out what is happening. Plan your first bay asks six questions about how work moves in your plant and suggests where to start.

Do the four need different hardware?

No. All four run on the same AmIGo tags, AmIMo anchors and AmIHub. A forklift, tugger or AMR takes the vehicle form of the tag.
