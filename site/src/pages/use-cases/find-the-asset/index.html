Common questions.

Does anyone scan the trolley to find it?

No. The anchors locate its tag whenever it moves. At rest, the map keeps its last position.

What if a tag goes quiet?

The map shows when it was last seen. It does not guess where the trolley is now.

What else can carry a tag?

Bins, kit racks, fixtures, tools, forklifts and tuggers.
