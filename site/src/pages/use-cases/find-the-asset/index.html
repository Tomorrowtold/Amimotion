Where is that trolley? Look it up.

Today someone walks the hall to find it. With an AmIGo tag on it, the trolley is a place on AmIHub's map.

With AmIMotion, every tagged trolley, bin, tool and fixture shows on one live map in AmIHub. AmIMo anchors place each tag down to ±10 cm, depending on your floor. You see where it is, or where it was last seen and when. Finding one starts with a search, not a walk.

Tools and vehicles

Today, and with AmIMotion.

  • Today.

    A worker needs one trolley. Nobody knows where it is. Someone walks the hall, calls stores and checks a clipboard. Then walks again.

  • With AmIMotion.

    The worker looks it up in AmIHub. The map shows the zone, and when the trolley was last seen. The worker walks straight there.

How a trolley stays findable.

  1. It moves.

    The AmIGo tag sends a short UWB pulse as the trolley moves. Nobody scans it.

  2. Anchors hear it.

    AmIMo anchors on your columns record when each pulse arrives, and the angle it came from.

  3. AmIHub places it.

    AmIHub works out where the tag is and puts it on the live map.

  4. It stops.

    The tag sleeps. AmIHub keeps its last-seen place and time.

Common questions.

Does anyone scan the trolley to find it?

No. AmIMo anchors hear its AmIGo tag whenever it moves. At rest, AmIHub keeps its last-seen place.

What if a tag goes quiet?

AmIHub shows where and when it was last seen. It does not guess where the trolley is now.

What else can carry a tag?

Bins, kit racks, fixtures, tool carts, forklifts and tuggers. Each one gets its own AmIGo tag in AmIHub.

How close does it place the trolley?

Down to ±10 cm with AmIMo anchors. How close depends on your floor, so the survey checks it.
