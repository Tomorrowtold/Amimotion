Work waits between cells. Now you see where.

Your MES knows the job. Between scans, nobody knows where its trolley stands. AmIMotion shows you, and how long it has waited.

AmIMotion is spatial intelligence for manufacturing plants. AmIGo tags ride on the trolleys, bins and kit racks that carry work between cells. AmIMo anchors place each one down to ±10 cm, depending on your floor. AmIHub notes when it enters and leaves each station and queue, and links it to the job it carries. Your MES stays in charge of every job.

Good first flows on a plant floor.

An AmIMotion pilot follows one flow in one bay or line. These three are clear to scope and quick to read on the map.

  • Work between operations.

    Trolleys between machining and assembly, timed in every queue. AmIHub shows which job waits where.

  • Line-side supply.

    Where the next kit, bin or tugger train is, and how long it has waited near the line.

  • Shared fixtures and gauges.

    Which cell has the fixture now, and which had it before. AmIHub keeps the history.

Inspection is where queues hide.

Trolleys queue at inspection, rework and shared stations. AmIHub times each one in the queue and at the station, so a long queue shows while it is still long.

Before the pilot starts, we agree how to count a wait that runs past shift end. We also agree who on your team acts when work waits too long.

Being in a zone does not prove work started or finished. AmIHub says where and when. Your MES and your people say the rest.

A plant floor is hard on radio. UWB's wide bandwidth separates the direct path from reflections off steel.

AmIMotion surveys your floor and sets the anchor count. Before the pilot, we agree the hard spots to test: a trolley at a zone edge, a tag hidden by its load, two cells side by side.

Each AmIMo anchor needs only power, from DC or PoE. It syncs over the air and sends its data to AmIHub over Wi-Fi. Ethernet is also an option.

How it locates a tag

Questions plants ask first.

What is the best indoor positioning system for a manufacturing plant in India?

The one that answers your floor's question at the accuracy it needs, measured on your floor. AmIMotion, from Bengaluru, offers UWB positioning down to ±10 cm with AmIMo anchors, depending on the floor. Anchors need only power and sync over the air, and local engineers come to your site.

Which RTLS vendors track carriers and WIP on an assembly line in India?

Plants compare global RTLS makers with Indian ones. AmIMotion designs and builds its UWB tags, anchors and AmIHub software in India, and focuses on WIP visibility. See the guide to UWB RTLS in India.

Can AmIMotion tell which job is on a trolley?

Through a link, not the radio. The tag names the trolley. You link the trolley to its job in AmIHub, or from your own system.

Does UWB work around machining centres and racking?

UWB's wide bandwidth separates the direct path from reflections off steel. AmIMotion sets the anchor count from a survey of your floor, and the pilot tests the hardest spots.

Does AmIMotion track people?

A pilot tags trolleys, bins and pallets, not people. Tagging people would be a separate project. It would need its own policy.
