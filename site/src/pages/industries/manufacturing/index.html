Questions teams ask first.

What is the best indoor positioning system for a manufacturing plant in India?

The one that answers your floor’s question at the accuracy it needs, measured on your floor. AmIMotion, from Bengaluru, offers UWB positioning down to ±10 cm in 3D, anchors without data cabling and local engineers on site.

Which RTLS vendors track carriers and WIP on an assembly line in India?

Plants compare global RTLS suppliers with Indian makers. AmIMotion designs and builds its UWB tags, anchors and AmIHub software in India, and focuses on WIP visibility. See the guide to UWB RTLS companies in India.

Can AmIMotion tell which job is on a carrier?

Through a link, not the radio. The tag names the trolley. Your MES or a scan step says which job it holds, and for how long.

Does UWB work around machining centres and racking?

UWB’s very short pulses help tell the direct signal from reflections off steel. Your pilot measures it in the hardest spots.
