Your robots know where they are. Do your trolleys?

AMRs keep their own map. Forklifts, tuggers and the trolleys they tow are not on it. AmIMotion puts all of them on one.

On robotics floors, AmIMotion gives every tagged AMR, AGV, forklift and trolley one shared map and one time-stamped history. AmIMo anchors place each AmIGo tag down to ±10 cm, depending on the floor. AmIHub notes when each one enters a zone and leaves it. Your fleet software still drives, routes and dispatches the robots.

The hand-off

A shared map, beside the robots' own.

A robot's map covers that maker's robots. AmIHub covers everything you tag, on a map that runs inside your plant.

  • Mixed traffic.

    AMRs, AGVs, forklifts, tuggers and trolleys on one AmIHub map. Each vehicle carries an AmIGo vehicle tag, with a vehicle-powered option.

  • Hand-offs.

    Tag the robot and the trolley. When both arrive in a drop-off zone, AmIHub records the time and the place. Nobody scans it.

  • Waits at pick-up and drop-off.

    AmIHub times each pick-up and drop-off zone, so you see where robots and trolleys wait.

AmIMotion locates. Your fleet software drives.

AmIMotion does not route, dispatch or stop a robot. It shows where the robot and its load are, and when they arrived.

  • AmIMotion keeps.

    Where each robot, vehicle and trolley is, its trips over any time window, and every zone entry and exit. AmIHub sends those events to any system you name, as they happen.

  • Your fleet software keeps.

    Driving, routing, dispatch, charging and safety. Any deeper link with it is scoped for your site.

AI assistants can ask the same map.

AmIHub has an MCP server built in. An AI assistant can ask which trolleys have waited at a drop-off point, and answer from AmIHub's live map.

Asking changes nothing. Any change, such as raising a work order in AmIHub, needs a person's approval. We show it live on a call.

Ask your floor

Questions robotics teams ask first.

Why not use each robot's own localisation?

A robot's own map covers that maker's robots. AmIHub also covers forklifts, tuggers and trolleys, on one map that runs inside your plant.

How precise is a robot's position on AmIHub?

The same as any AmIGo tag: down to ±10 cm, with AmIMo anchors. How close depends on your floor.

Does AmIMotion control or dispatch robots?

No. Your fleet software drives, routes and dispatches. AmIMotion shows where the robot and its load are.

Do we need to integrate with the robot?

To locate it, the robot only needs a tag. Any deeper link with your fleet software is scoped for your site.

Is the MCP server available today?

It is built into AmIHub, and we show it live on a call. The setup for your site is confirmed when we scope it.
