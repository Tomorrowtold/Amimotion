Questions teams ask first.

Why not use each robot’s own localisation?

A robot’s own map covers that vendor’s robots. A shared layer also covers forklifts, trolleys and people, in data you own.

How precise is a robot’s position?

The same as any tag: down to ±10 cm in 3D, with AmIMo anchors. Your site sets the final figure.

Is the MCP server available today?

It is built into AmIHub and shown in a live demo. The setup for your site is confirmed during scoping.
