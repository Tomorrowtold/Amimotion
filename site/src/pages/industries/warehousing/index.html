Questions teams ask first.

Can AmIMotion track forklifts and AMRs together?

Yes. Tags fit forklifts, tuggers and robots, and all of them appear on one live map.

Does it replace our WMS?

No. AmIHub feeds your WMS over REST, MQTT, WebSocket or omlox. The WMS stays the record.

Can AmIMotion time dock-to-stock?

Yes. Each tagged pallet or tote is time-stamped as it enters and leaves the dock, staging lanes and put-away zones, so you see where loads wait.
