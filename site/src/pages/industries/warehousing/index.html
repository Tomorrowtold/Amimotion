Know where each pallet is. And how long it has waited.

Your WMS knows where a pallet should be. AmIMotion shows where it is, and how long it has stood there.

In warehousing, AmIMotion shows where tagged pallets, totes, forklifts and tuggers are, and how long loads wait at docks and staging lanes. AmIGo tags ride on the loads and vehicles. AmIMo anchors place each tag down to ±10 cm, depending on the floor. AmIHub times every entry and exit, and your WMS stays the record for orders and stock.

Good first flows in a warehouse.

An AmIMotion pilot follows one flow, such as inbound from the dock to the rack. These three are where warehouses look first.

  • Docks and staging lanes.

    AmIHub times each tagged pallet at every dock door and staging lane. You see which load has waited longest, while it waits.

  • Forklifts and tuggers.

    A vehicle tag rides on each one, with a vehicle-powered option. AmIHub shows where each one is now, and its trips over any time window.

  • Pallets and totes.

    Find a tagged load through put-away, staging and storage. AmIHub shows where it was last seen, and when.

Dock to stock, timed.

Name the dock, the staging lane and the put-away zone in AmIHub. It notes when each tagged pallet enters and leaves each one.

  1. Dock.

    A tagged pallet enters the dock zone. AmIHub records the time.

  2. Staging lane.

    It waits in a lane. Its wait shows live, during the shift.

  3. Put-away.

    A forklift picks it up. Both tags move together toward the racks.

  4. Rack.

    It leaves the last zone. AmIHub sends each entry and exit to your WMS, as it happens.

Docks, aisles and covered yards.

  • Docks and gates.

    AmIMo anchors can use angle and two-way ranging at docks and gates. The mode is set per zone in AmIHub, with no hardware swap.

  • Tall racking.

    Racking changes how far a signal reaches. AmIMotion surveys your floor and sets the anchor count.

  • Covered yards.

    AmIMo anchors also work in sheltered outdoor areas, so a covered yard can join the same map.

Questions warehouses ask first.

Can AmIMotion track forklifts and AMRs together?

Yes. AmIGo vehicle tags fit forklifts, tuggers and AMRs, and all of them appear on one AmIHub map with the pallets they move.

Does AmIMotion replace our WMS?

No. AmIHub sends positions and events to your WMS over REST, MQTT, WebSocket or omlox. Your WMS stays the record. The connector for your WMS is confirmed when we scope the pilot.

Can AmIMotion time dock-to-stock?

Yes, for tagged pallets and totes. AmIHub notes each entry and exit at the dock, the staging lanes and the put-away zones, so you see where loads wait.

Do the anchors need data cables?

No data or sync cable. Each AmIMo anchor needs only power, from DC or PoE, syncs over the air and sends its data over Wi-Fi. Ethernet is also an option.
