AmIGo, in short

AmIGo is AmIMotion's UWB tag for trolleys, bins, tools, fixtures and vehicles. A motion sensor wakes it to send a short ultra-wideband pulse; when it stops, it sleeps, so one battery lasts 1 to 3 years, typical and depending on use. With AmIMo anchors, AmIHub places an AmIGo tag down to ±10 cm in 3D. How close depends on your floor.

Built to be forgotten. Fit it once. Find it always.

A tag earns its place by never needing attention. AmIGo stays quiet until something moves, and AmIHub tells you before one needs a battery.

  • Down to ±10 cm, in 3D. With AmIMo anchors, AmIHub places each tag closely enough to tell one trolley from the next. How close depends on your floor.

  • Speaks when it moves. Sleeps when it stops. A 3-axis accelerometer wakes it. While it sleeps, the map keeps where it was last seen, and when.

  • You set the pace. From once every ten seconds to ten times a second, per group. Busy tags at high rates use more battery.

  • No surprises. AmIHub tracks each tag's health and battery, so you plan a change before a tag goes flat.

  • It feels the knocks. The same accelerometer reports tilt and impact events, not just motion.

  • Three forms, one radio. An asset tag, a vehicle tag that can run off the vehicle, and a personnel badge.

When a tag goes quiet

On the floor, it disappears. On the map, it never does.

One tag. Fitted three ways.

  1. On a carrier. Trolleys, bins, racks and WIP carriers. AmIGo screws on through its two moulded ears, or sticks on.

  2. On a vehicle. Forklifts, tuggers and AGVs. It sits in a vehicle cradle and can run off the vehicle's power.

  3. On a tool. Fixtures, gauges and hand tools. Find the tool from a screen, and see which cell had it last.

  • Which job is on the trolley? Linking trolleys to jobs

The tag names the trolley. You name the job.

An AmIGo tag tells AmIHub which trolley it is on, not which job the trolley carries. Link each trolley to its job, in AmIHub or from your own system, and its wait counts as that job's wait. When the trolley takes new work, the old link ends before the new one starts.

Questions about AmIGo.

What is an AmIGo tag?

AmIGo is AmIMotion's UWB tag for trolleys, bins, tools, fixtures and vehicles. With AmIMo anchors, AmIHub places it down to ±10 cm in 3D, depending on your floor. Its battery lasts 1 to 3 years, typical.

What battery life does a UWB asset tag have?

An AmIGo UWB tag lasts 1 to 3 years on one battery, typical. It depends on how often the tag moves and the update rate you set, because AmIGo sleeps when it stops.

Is AmIGo small enough for totes and hand tools?

AmIGo is a compact, low-profile tag that screws or sticks on. It is made for bins, totes, tools and fixtures as well as trolleys. Its exact size is in the datasheet, shared under NDA.

Does every AmIGo tag report at the same rate?

No. You set the rate per tag group, from 0.1 to 10 times a second. Each tag reports only while it moves.

Can AmIGo tag people?

AmIGo also comes as a lanyard or helmet badge. A pilot tags trolleys, bins and pallets, not people. Tagging people would be a separate project. It would need its own policy.
