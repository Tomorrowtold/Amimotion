AmIGo tag. It rides on what moves.

A small UWB tag that screws or sticks onto a trolley, bin, tool or vehicle. It sends a short pulse when it moves and sleeps when it stops.

AmIGo, in short.

AmIGo is AmIMotion's UWB tag for trolleys, bins, tools, fixtures and vehicles. A motion sensor wakes it to send a short ultra-wideband pulse. When it stops, it sleeps, so one battery lasts 1 to 3 years, typical and depending on use. With AmIMo anchors, AmIHub places an AmIGo tag down to ±10 cm in 3D. How close depends on your floor.

Speaks when it moves. Sleeps when it stops.

Where the battery life comes from.

A 3-axis accelerometer wakes AmIGo when the trolley moves. When it stops, the tag goes into deep sleep. While it sleeps, the map shows where it was last seen, and when.

You set how often each group of tags reports, from once every ten seconds to ten times a second. Busy tags at high rates use more battery. AmIHub tracks each tag's health and battery level, so you plan a change before a tag goes flat.

Fewer pulses. Longer life.
  • AmIGo sends only when its accelerometer says it is moving.
  • You set the update rate per tag group, from 0.1 to 10 times a second.
  • 1 to 3 years on one battery is typical. Busy tags at high rates use more.
  • Forklift and tugger tags can run off the vehicle instead.

When a tag goes quiet

One tag. Fitted three ways.

  1. On a carrier

    For trolleys, bins, racks and WIP carriers. AmIGo screws on through its two moulded ears, or sticks on.

  2. On a vehicle

    For forklifts, tuggers and AGVs. It sits in a vehicle cradle and can run off the vehicle's power.

  3. On a tool

    For fixtures, gauges and hand tools. Find the tool from a screen, and see which cell had it last.

  • Which job is on the trolley? Linking trolleys to jobs

The tag names the trolley. You name the job.

An AmIGo tag tells AmIHub which trolley it is on, not which job the trolley carries. Link each trolley to the job it carries, in AmIHub or from your own system. Its wait then counts as that job's wait. When the trolley takes new work, the old link ends before the new one starts.

Questions about AmIGo.

What is an AmIGo tag?

AmIGo is AmIMotion's UWB tag for trolleys, bins, tools, fixtures and vehicles. With AmIMo anchors, AmIHub places it down to ±10 cm in 3D, depending on your floor. Its battery lasts 1 to 3 years, typical.

What battery life does a UWB asset tag have?

An AmIGo UWB tag lasts 1 to 3 years on one battery, typical. It depends on how often the tag moves and the update rate you set, because AmIGo sleeps when it stops.

Is AmIGo small enough for totes and hand tools?

AmIGo is a compact, low-profile tag that screws or sticks on. It is made for bins, totes, tools and fixtures as well as trolleys. Its exact size is in the datasheet, shared under NDA.

Does every AmIGo tag report at the same rate?

No. You set the rate per tag group, from 0.1 to 10 times a second. Each tag reports only while it moves.

Can AmIGo tag people?

AmIGo also comes as a lanyard or helmet badge. A pilot tags trolleys, bins and pallets, not people. Tagging people would be a separate project. It would need its own policy.
