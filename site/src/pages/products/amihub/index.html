AmIHub, in short.

AmIHub is AmIMotion's software: the positioning engine and the record of where every tag has been. It works out each AmIGo tag's position from AmIMo timing and angle, and keeps a live map of your floor and its zones. AmIHub notes when each tagged item enters and leaves a zone, so you see how long it has waited, while it is still waiting. It runs on an industrial PC inside your plant with no internet needed, or in the cloud. It speaks REST, MQTT, WebSocket and omlox, and has an MCP server built in.

What AmIHub does.

  • Works out positions

    From each pulse's arrival times and angles, filtered against your floor map. Positions update in under a second, indicative.

  • Keeps the live map

    Your floor plan, zones and racking, with every tagged item, its trail and where it was last seen.

  • Times every zone

    AmIHub time-stamps each entry and exit. You name the zones. You see how long each item has waited.

  • Answers by time window

    Ask for any item's trail, trips and waits over the hours you choose. Export to CSV or Parquet.

  • Looks after the devices

    Finds each anchor on its own, tracks tag health and battery, updates firmware over the air, and raises an alert if an anchor goes dark.

  • Grows with you

    From one bay to the whole plant on the same AmIHub, without re-fitting the first bay. Thousands of tags per site, indicative.

Questions about AmIHub.

What is AmIHub?

AmIHub is AmIMotion's software. It is the positioning engine, the live map of your floor and the record of every zone entry and exit. It has open APIs (REST, MQTT, WebSocket, omlox) and a built-in MCP server, and runs on-premise or in the cloud.

Which RTLS can run on-premise without internet?

AmIHub, AmIMotion's software, runs on an industrial PC inside your plant with no internet needed, so location data stays on site. It can also run in the cloud if your IT rules prefer that.

Does AmIHub replace our MES or WMS?

No. Your MES stays in charge of every job. AmIHub adds where its trolley is and how long it has waited, and feeds your MES, ERP or WMS over open interfaces.

Can AmIHub send zone events to SAP or an MES over MQTT?

AmIHub sends each zone entry and exit over MQTT, as it happens, and offers REST, WebSocket and an omlox hub interface. The connector for your SAP, MES or ERP is confirmed for your site.

Does AmIMotion have an MCP server?

Yes. AmIHub has an MCP server built in, so AI assistants that support MCP can ask the live map a question. Any change needs a person's approval. We show it live on a call.
