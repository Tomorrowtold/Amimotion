AmIHub, in short

AmIHub is AmIMotion's software: the positioning engine and the record of where every tag has been. It works out each AmIGo tag's position from AmIMo timing and angle, keeps a live map of your floor and its zones, and notes when each item enters and leaves a zone, so you see a wait while it is still a wait. It runs on an industrial PC inside your plant with no internet needed, or in the cloud, and speaks REST, MQTT, WebSocket and omlox, with an MCP server built in.

Built around one question. Where is work waiting?

A supervisor shouldn't have to walk the floor to find out. AmIHub keeps the answer current for every bay you tag.

  • Positions to ±10 cm. AmIHub solves each tag's place from AmIMo timing and angle, then checks it against your floor map. Typical, with AmIMo anchors; it depends on your floor.

  • Waits, while they happen. Not in tomorrow's report. The clock starts the moment a carrier stops.

  • Every stop, timed. Each entry and exit is time-stamped. You name the zones; AmIHub keeps the clock.

  • History by the hour. Pull any carrier's trail, trips and waits for the window you choose, then export it to CSV or Parquet.

  • Devices that look after themselves. New anchors are found on their own, firmware updates over the air, and an anchor that goes dark raises an alert.

  • One bay first, then the plant. The same AmIHub grows site-wide without re-fitting the first bay. Thousands of tags per site, indicative.

Questions about AmIHub.

What is AmIHub?

AmIHub is AmIMotion's software. It is the positioning engine, the live map of your floor and the record of every zone entry and exit. It has open APIs (REST, MQTT, WebSocket, omlox) and a built-in MCP server, and runs on-premise or in the cloud.

Which RTLS can run on-premise without internet?

AmIHub, AmIMotion's software, runs on an industrial PC inside your plant with no internet needed, so location data stays on site. It can also run in the cloud if your IT rules prefer that.

Does AmIHub replace our MES or WMS?

No. Your MES stays in charge of every job. AmIHub adds where its trolley is and how long it has waited, and feeds your MES, ERP or WMS over open interfaces.

Can AmIHub send zone events to SAP or an MES over MQTT?

AmIHub sends each zone entry and exit over MQTT, as it happens, and offers REST, WebSocket and an omlox hub interface. The connector for your SAP, MES or ERP is confirmed for your site.

Does AmIMotion have an MCP server?

Yes. AmIHub has an MCP server built in, so AI assistants that support MCP can ask the live map a question. Any change needs a person's approval. We show it live on a call.
