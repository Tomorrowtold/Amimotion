Interfaces

AmIHub, in short.

AmIHub is AmIMotion’s positioning engine and system of record. It keeps a live map of zones and tracked items, time-stamps every enter, exit, idle and dwell event, and shares them over REST, MQTT, WebSocket and an omlox hub interface. It runs on an industrial PC in your plant with no internet dependency, or in the cloud, and has a built-in MCP server for AI assistants.

  • Positioning engine

    TDoA, AoA and two-way ranging solvers. Non-line-of-sight algorithms keep positions steady around racking and metal.

  • Live map

    Your floor as a 3D model: plan, zones, geofences and racking, with every tracked item, its trail and history.

  • Zones and events

    Every enter, exit, idle and dwell is time-stamped and queryable, for assets, vehicles, people and work orders.

  • History

    Trajectory, trip and dwell queries by time window. CSV and Parquet export.

  • Devices

    Auto-discovery, health and battery monitoring and over-the-air firmware, from one console.

  • Scale

    From one bay to many sites. Thousands of tags and hundreds of anchors per site, indicative.

Questions teams ask first.

What is AmIHub?

AmIHub is AmIMotion’s RTLS software: the positioning engine, live 3D map, time-stamped zone events and open APIs (REST, MQTT, WebSocket, omlox and a built-in MCP server). It runs on-premise or in the cloud.

Which RTLS can run on-premise without internet?

AmIHub runs on an industrial PC inside your plant with no internet dependency. Location data stays on site. A cloud option is there if you prefer it.

Does AmIHub replace our MES or WMS?

No. AmIHub adds where and when. Jobs, orders and status stay in your MES, ERP or WMS, which AmIHub feeds over open interfaces.

Does AmIHub integrate with SAP or MES over MQTT?

AmIHub publishes zone events over MQTT and offers REST, WebSocket and an omlox hub interface. The connector for your SAP, MES or ERP is confirmed during scoping.

Does AmIMotion have an MCP server?

Yes, AmIHub has a built-in MCP server, so MCP-capable AI assistants can query live location data, with a person’s approval for any action. Shown in a live demo.
