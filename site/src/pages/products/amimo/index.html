AmIMo in short

AmIMo is AmIMotion’s UWB anchor. A 3D angle-of-arrival antenna array reads direction as well as arrival time, so as few as two anchors in view can fix a position. The same unit offers BLE ranging and Wi-Fi FTM. Each anchor reaches about 50 m in line of sight (indicative, site-dependent) and needs only DC or PoE power.

Questions teams ask first.

What is an AmIMo anchor?

AmIMo is AmIMotion’s UWB anchor. It times each tag pulse and reads its 3D angle of arrival, offers BLE and Wi-Fi FTM from the same unit, and needs only DC or PoE power, with no data or sync cabling.

Does an RTLS anchor need data cabling?

Not AmIMo. It syncs over the air and sends data over Wi-Fi, with an Ethernet option. Each anchor still needs power, from DC or PoE.

How far does one AmIMo anchor reach?

About 50 m in line of sight, indicative and site-dependent. Racking, machines and metal reduce the useful range.

How many anchors does a floor need?

It depends on the accuracy you need, ceiling height and how much racking blocks line of sight. Angle of arrival lets as few as two anchors in view fix a position. A site survey sets the count.

Can AmIMo work with phones?

AmIMo offers Wi-Fi FTM ranging from the same anchors, and in downlink mode a device works out its own position, which keeps it on the device. Which phones and laptops are supported depends on their chipset, so it is confirmed for each site.
