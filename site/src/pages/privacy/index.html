Everything on this site works without signing up or handing over contact details.

Advertising and tracking

This site has no advertising pixels and no cross-site tracking. The host may process technical request data to serve and secure the site.

Floor diagnostic

Your answers stay in this browser tab, so you can pick up where you left off. They leave your browser only if you choose to send your profile.

Tools and pilot briefs

The anchor estimator, search-time calculator and pilot planner run in your browser. Downloading a brief saves a local file. Nothing is sent to AmIMotion unless you email it.

Enquiries

The contact form prepares an email draft in your own mail app. Sending is a step you take.

Questions

For questions about information you send to AmIMotion, write to sales@amimotion.com. This page covers this website only.
