Privacy notice.

This notice covers the amimotion.com website. It says what the site collects, what it does not, and what you can ask of us.

The AmIMotion website has no sign-up, no advertising trackers and no analytics. The planning tools and Plan your first bay run in your browser. Your answers to Plan your first bay sit in this browser tab only, and nothing reaches AmIMotion until you send an email. When you write to us, Locatepro Technologies Pvt Ltd uses your details to reply and to follow up the enquiry you started. You can ask to see, correct or erase them under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Trust and data·People and privacy

Who we are

This website is run by Locatepro Technologies Pvt Ltd, Bengaluru, India. AmIMotion is its brand. For personal data you send us through this site, we are the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Write to us at sales@amimotion.com.

What the site does not do

There is no sign-up and no account. The site sets no cookies. It loads no advertising, analytics or social-media trackers. It loads no fonts or scripts from other companies' servers.

What stays in your browser

Plan your first bay keeps your answers in this browser tab, so you can go back a step. They are deleted when you close the tab, and Start over clears them at once. If you type a work email there, the page guesses your company name from it inside your browser. How many anchors you need, Time spent searching and Your pilot on one page work out their answers in your browser and keep nothing. A brief you download is a file on your own device. Copy puts text on your own clipboard.

When you send us something

The contact form and Plan your first bay open a draft email in your own mail app, addressed to sales@amimotion.com. Nothing reaches us until you press send. When you do, we receive what the email contains: usually your name, work email and company, your role and phone number if you gave them, and your answers. We use it only to reply to you and to follow up the enquiry you started. We do not sell it, and we do not add you to a newsletter.

How long we keep it

We keep an enquiry for as long as we need it to reply and to follow up, then erase it, unless a law requires us to keep it longer.

Server logs

The company that hosts this site may record standard request data, such as your IP address, browser type, the page asked for and the time, to serve the site and keep it secure. We do not use it to build a profile of you.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it. You can ask us to correct, complete, update or erase it. You can name someone to act for you if you die or cannot act yourself. You can withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, by writing to us. Withdrawing does not undo what was lawfully done before.

Complaints

Write to sales@amimotion.com first, and we will answer you. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

This site is for businesses. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect a child's personal data.

Other sites

A link to another website, such as a video channel, takes you to that site's own privacy terms.
