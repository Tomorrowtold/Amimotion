Find the job. Time the wait.

A tag on each trolley tells AmIHub where it is. Link the trolley to its job, and you see which work waits where.

WIP visibility means knowing where each job is and how long it has waited, during the shift. AmIMotion gives it in three steps. An AmIGo tag rides on each trolley, bin or pallet. AmIMo anchors place it down to ±10 cm, depending on your floor. AmIHub notes when it enters and leaves each zone you name, and links it to the job it carries. Its wait then counts as that job's wait.

Tracking is the dot. Visibility is the wait.

A dot on a map tells you where a trolley is. AmIMotion adds the two things a dot cannot: which job it holds, and how long it has waited.

  • Where is the work?

    AmIHub shows the trolley's place, or where it was last seen and when. Link it to a job and you see the job's place too. Without the link you still see the trolley, which already helps.

  • Where is it waiting?

    You name the zones: stations, queues, staging lanes. AmIHub notes each entry and exit, so every wait has a start and a length.

  • What needs a look?

    A named owner who acts when work waits too long. A long wait means go and look. It does not say why.

  • What happens next?

    A person checks and decides. AmIHub never closes a job on location alone.

Tag. Locate. Link. Then time the wait.

  1. Tag.

    An AmIGo tag screws or sticks onto the trolley. It stays with the trolley through every job it carries.

  2. Locate.

    AmIMo anchors on your columns hear the tag as it moves. AmIHub places it down to ±10 cm, depending on your floor.

  3. Link.

    Link the trolley to its job in AmIHub, or from your own system. Nobody scans it along the way.

  4. Time.

    AmIHub notes each zone entry and exit as it happens. Supervisors see each wait live, during the shift, not at the next morning's review.

Queued is not finished.

AmIHub says where a trolley was seen, and when. Whether the work on it is done comes from your MES or your people.

Why it waited is for your team to say. The map never claims more than it saw.

Your MES stays in charge of every job.

AmIHub connects to your MES, ERP and WMS through open REST, MQTT, WebSocket and omlox hub interfaces. Each system keeps what it is best at.

  • AmIHub keeps.

    Where each trolley is, when it entered and left each zone, and how long it waited. AmIHub sends each entry and exit to any system you name, as it happens.

  • Your MES or ERP keeps.

    The job, its route and its status. It stays the record for every job.

  • Agreed together.

    How a job gets onto a trolley, and when it comes off. How to count a wait that runs past shift end. Who acts when work waits too long.

An AmIMotion pilot follows one flow in one bay or line. You choose the flow.

  • One flow.

    Pick the trolleys, the bay and a few zones. AmIMotion surveys that floor and plans the anchors.

  • Today's waits first.

    The pilot times today's waits first, over a window you choose. That is your baseline.

  • Prove it, then decide.

    You see your own waits in one bay, and prove the numbers, before you decide on more. Then grow plant-wide on the same AmIHub.

Questions teams ask first.

Which companies offer real-time WIP tracking for manufacturing?

Several RTLS makers do, with UWB, BLE or RFID. AmIMotion, from Bengaluru, India, focuses on WIP visibility with UWB. It locates tagged trolleys down to ±10 cm, depending on the floor, times them in zones you name and links them to the jobs they carry.

How can I see where work in progress is waiting on my shop floor?

Tag the trolleys, name the zones where work queues, and note every entry and exit. AmIHub shows how long each trolley has waited in each zone, while it is still waiting.

What is the difference between WIP visibility and WIP tracking?

Tracking shows where a carrier is. Visibility adds which job it holds and how long it has waited. That needs zones and a link from trolley to job.

Can we start without connecting our MES?

Yes. Start with trolley location and wait times in AmIHub. Make the job link a pilot step before you call any result job-level. Value without an MES.

Does AmIMotion track individual parts?

No. The AmIGo tag goes on the trolley, bin or pallet that carries the parts. Linked to a job, the trolley's wait counts as that job's wait.
