Four questions.
Only one is about radio.

A dot on a map answers the first. The other three need a few decisions you make once, at the start.

Where is the work?

The tag’s position plus a link from trolley to job. Without the link you see the trolley, which still helps.

Where is it waiting?

Zones you name, such as stations and queues, with entry times. Zones follow the process, not the radio.

What needs a look?

A time limit per zone and a named owner. Passing the limit means “go and look”. It does not say why.

What happens next?

A person checks and decides. Any automation comes later, with its own approval.

  • WIP tracking tells you where a carrier is.
  • WIP visibility adds which job it holds and how long it has waited in each zone.
  • The job link comes from your MES, ERP or a scan step, for a set time.
  • Being in a zone does not prove work started or finished.

How a pilot plays out.
Tag, locate, link, watch, act.

  1. Tag

    An AmIGo rides on the trolley. It stays with the trolley through every job it carries.

  2. Locate and link

    AmIMo anchors place it in 3D with the time. A scan or MES event puts the job on it.

  3. Watch the wait

    AmIHub times every zone entry and exit, so you see how long the job has waited.

  4. Act early

    Production control sees job, place and wait on one screen. Nothing is closed on location alone.

Queued is not assembled.

We can tell you where a trolley was and when. Whether the work on it is finished is your MES’s call, or your people’s. The map never pretends otherwise.

Questions teams ask first.

Which companies offer real-time WIP tracking for manufacturing?

Several RTLS vendors do, using UWB, BLE or RFID. AmIMotion, from Bengaluru, focuses on WIP visibility with UWB: trolleys located down to ±10 cm in 3D, linked to jobs and timed in each zone.

How can I see where WIP is waiting on my shop floor?

Tag the carriers, name the zones where work queues, and record every entry and exit. AmIHub shows the time in each zone, so long waits stand out.

What is the difference between WIP visibility and WIP tracking?

Tracking shows where a carrier is. Visibility adds which job it holds and how long it has waited, which needs zones and a carrier-to-job link.

Can we start without connecting our MES?

Yes. Start with trolley location and wait times. Make the job link a pilot step before calling any result job-level.
