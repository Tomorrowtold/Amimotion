Two questions. One system.

Where is it? How long has it waited? AmIMotion answers both, for anything you tag.

AmIMotion is spatial intelligence for factories and warehouses. It answers two questions on one live map in AmIHub. Where is work waiting, and for how long? And where is the trolley, tool or forklift someone needs right now? AmIGo tags ride on what moves. AmIMo anchors on your columns hear them. AmIHub places each tag down to ±10 cm. How close depends on your floor.

What AmIMotion leaves to your systems.

AmIMotion adds where and how long. It does not take over the records you already trust.

  • Your MES or WMS keeps the job.

    AmIHub adds where its trolley or pallet is and how long it has waited. Orders, routes and status stay where they are.

  • Your team says why.

    AmIHub shows that a load waited and for how long. Why it waited is for your team to say.

  • Your fleet software drives.

    AmIMotion locates robots and vehicles. It does not route, dispatch or stop them.

Questions teams ask first.

What does AmIMotion solve on a factory floor?

Two things. AmIMotion shows where work in progress waits, and for how long, by timing tagged trolleys and pallets in zones you name. It also finds tagged trolleys, tools and vehicles from a screen, with where and when each was last seen.

Do we have to choose one?

No. Both run on the same AmIGo tags, AmIMo anchors and AmIHub. Start with one flow in one bay. Then grow plant-wide on the same AmIHub, without re-fitting the first bay.

Does AmIMotion track people?

A pilot tags trolleys, bins and pallets, not people. Tagging people would be a separate project. It would need its own policy.
