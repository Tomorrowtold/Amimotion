Two questions. One system.

Where is it? And how long has it waited? AmIMotion answers both on one live map.

AmIMotion is a UWB real-time location system for manufacturing. Teams use it for two jobs: work-in-progress visibility, which shows where jobs wait between operations, and finding tagged tools, trolleys and vehicles from a screen. Positions are accurate down to ±10 cm in 3D, with AmIMo anchors.
