Find it from a screen. Not a walk.

Tag the fixtures, tool carts, trolleys and vehicles your team looks for. AmIHub shows where each one is, or where it was last seen.

To cut the time spent searching for trolleys, bins and tools, AmIMotion puts a small AmIGo tag on each one. AmIMo anchors on your columns hear it as it moves. AmIHub's live map shows where each one is now, which zone had it last, and when it was last seen. Forklifts, tuggers and AMRs carry a vehicle tag on the same map.

Where was it last seen?

Things that move all day get lost all day. AmIHub keeps a last-seen place and time for every AmIGo tag, so the search starts on a screen.

  • Tools and fixtures.

    A small AmIGo tag screws on where work won't knock it. AmIHub keeps a history of which cell had each fixture, and when.

  • Trolleys, bins and kit racks.

    Search AmIHub for the trolley. You see its zone and when it was last seen. Walk straight there.

  • Forklifts, tuggers and AMRs.

    A vehicle tag rides on each one, with a vehicle-powered option. AmIHub shows where each vehicle is, and its trips over any time window.

When a tag goes quiet.

An AmIGo tag sends a short UWB pulse when it moves and sleeps when it stops. While it sleeps, AmIHub shows where it was last seen, and when. It never guesses where the item is now.

If a tag comes off, the map still shows its last-seen spot. AmIHub tracks each tag's health and battery level. One battery lasts 1 to 3 years, depending on use.

Questions teams ask first.

How do I reduce time spent searching for trolleys and bins in a plant?

Tag what people search for and put it on a live map. With AmIMotion the search starts in AmIHub, with the zone and the last-seen time, instead of a walk around the hall. Count today's search time first, so you can show what changed.

How do you track tools on a factory floor?

Fix an AmIGo tag to the tool, fixture or gauge case with adhesive or a screw. AmIMo anchors hear it as it moves. AmIHub keeps a history of which cell had it, and when.

How do you track forklifts and AMRs indoors to within a few centimetres?

Fit each one with an AmIGo vehicle tag. AmIMo anchors place it down to ±10 cm, depending on your floor, on the same AmIHub map as trolleys and tools.

What happens if an RTLS tag is not seen for a while?

AmIHub shows where and when it was last seen. It does not guess where the item is now.

Hamare plant mein forklifts aur trolleys ka pata nahi chalta. Iska kya solution hai?

Forklifts aur trolleys par AmIMotion ka AmIGo UWB tag lagaiye. AmIMo anchors unhe ±10 cm tak locate karte hain, floor ke hisaab se. AmIHub ke live map par dikhta hai ki har ek abhi kahan hai, ya aakhri baar kahan aur kab dikha.
