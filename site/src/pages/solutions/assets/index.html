Tag the tools, fixtures, carriers and vehicles your team looks for. Find each one in seconds.

Questions teams ask first.

How do I reduce time spent searching for trolleys and bins?

Tag what people search for and put it on a live map. With AmIMotion the search starts on a screen, with the zone and the last-seen time, instead of a walk around the hall.

How do you track tools on a factory floor?

Fix an AmIGo tag to the tool, fixture or gauge case with adhesive or a screw. AmIMo anchors locate it, and AmIHub keeps a history of which cell had it.

Hamare plant mein forklifts aur trolleys ka pata nahi chalta. Iska kya solution hai?

Forklifts aur trolleys par AmIGo UWB tag lagaiye. AmIMo anchors unhe ±10 cm tak locate karte hain, aur AmIHub map par dikhta hai ki har ek abhi kahan hai aur kab se wahan khada hai.

What if a tag goes quiet?

The map shows when it was last seen. It does not guess where the item is now.
