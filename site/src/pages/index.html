Industrial spatial intelligence

AmIMotion builds industrial spatial intelligence: precise, time-stamped location of everything that moves in a plant, joined with jobs and zones, so people, systems and AI assistants can answer where work is and where it waits. One tag. Every carrier.
One system, from the tag to your AI.

One team builds every layer, so there is one contract and one support line.

  1. TagsAmIGo

    On trolleys, bins, pallets, totes, tools, forklifts and badges. Sends a pulse when it moves, so one battery lasts 1 to 3 years.

  2. AnchorsAmIMo

    On columns and walls, 3 to 8 m up. Times each pulse and reads its angle. Needs only power, DC or PoE.

  3. SoftwareAmIHub

    Turns pulses into positions down to ±10 cm in 3D, then into zones and waits.

  4. Your systemsMES, ERP, WMS and AI assistants

    REST, MQTT, WebSocket and an omlox hub. A built-in MCP server for AI assistants.
