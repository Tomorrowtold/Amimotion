Resources. Plan a location project, with no sign-up.

AmIMotion's resources help a plant plan real-time location before the first call: a six-question floor diagnostic, an anchor estimator, a search-time calculator, a one-page pilot planner, fourteen field guides, an FAQ and a glossary. None of them asks for an email. Each figure carries its condition, such as positions down to ±10 cm in 3D with AmIMo anchors, depending on the site.

Tools that work it out.

Each one runs in your browser. Nothing is stored and nobody asks for an email.

  • Plan your first bay

    Six questions about how work moves on your floor. AmIMotion's diagnostic sketches your floor as you answer and ends with a visibility profile and a pilot scope for one bay or line.

  • How many anchors you need

    Your floor size and racking in, a first anchor range out. AmIMotion's estimator shows its planning grid, and a site survey sets the real number.

  • Time spent searching

    Your people, searches and minutes, multiplied into hours a year. AmIMotion's calculator assumes no savings.

  • Your pilot on one page

    Three choices become a one-page brief for an AmIMotion pilot, to share with your team or bring to the first call.

Pages that explain it.

  • Field guides

    Fourteen guides, one question each: UWB vs RFID, anchor counts, accuracy, dwell time, job links and choosing an RTLS in India.

  • Questions

    Short answers about AmIMotion: the company, the three parts, accuracy, cabling, data, standards and AI assistants.

  • Glossary

    39 terms you hear when people talk about location on a factory floor, from RTLS and UWB to dwell and last seen.

Where to begin.

  1. Name the flow

    Pick the flow where people walk to find work. AmIMotion's diagnostic helps you choose it.

  2. Size the floor

    Get a first anchor range from the estimator, before anyone visits.

  3. Count the cost

    Put your own search time into hours a year, as the start of a baseline.

  4. Bring a brief

    Turn it into a one-page AmIMotion pilot brief for the first call.
