Three parts. One live map.

A tag rides on what moves. Anchors on your columns hear it. AmIHub shows where it is and how long it has waited.

AmIMotion is spatial intelligence for factories and warehouses, built from three parts. AmIGo tags ride on trolleys, bins, tools and vehicles. AmIMo anchors, fixed high on columns and walls, record when each tag's UWB pulse arrives and the angle it came from. AmIHub software works out each position and keeps one live map that your team, your systems and AI assistants all read. With AmIMo anchors, AmIHub places a tag down to ±10 cm in 3D. How close depends on your floor.

What each part does.

  • AmIGo tag

    Screws or sticks onto the trolley. Sends a short pulse when it moves and sleeps when it stops.

  • AmIMo anchor

    Fixed to a column 3 to 8 m up. Needs only power, from DC or PoE.

  • AmIHub

    Works out where each tag is and keeps the live map, on a PC inside your plant.

How a position is made.

  1. The tag sends.

    An AmIGo tag sends a UWB pulse a few nanoseconds long when it moves. When it stops, it sleeps.

  2. Anchors listen.

    Each AmIMo anchor in range records when the pulse arrives and the angle it came from.

  3. AmIHub works it out.

    AmIHub turns those times and angles into a 3D position on the live map, stamped with the time it was seen.

  4. Your systems hear.

    AmIHub sends each zone entry and exit to the systems you name, as it happens.

Who keeps what.

AmIHub records where and when. Your MES stays in charge of every job.

  • AmIHub keeps

    Each tag, its position, the time it was seen, and every zone it entered and left.

  • Your MES or ERP keeps

    The job, the batch, the routing and the status. Location alone does not mark work as done.

  • You link the two

    Link each trolley to the job it carries, in AmIHub or from your own system. Its wait then counts as that job's wait.

Specifications

Open at every layer.

AmIMotion is built on the FiRa and omlox specifications. AmIHub speaks REST, MQTT and WebSocket, and has an omlox hub interface, so your MES, ERP and WMS connect to it through open interfaces.

AmIHub also has an MCP server built in. Any AI assistant that supports MCP can ask the live map a question in plain words. Asking changes nothing. Any change needs a person's approval. We show it live on a call.

Questions about the platform.

What are the parts of AmIMotion?

Three. AmIGo tags ride on trolleys, bins, tools and vehicles. AmIMo anchors, fixed 3 to 8 m up, hear each tag. AmIHub software works out where each tag is and keeps the live map.

Does ±10 cm mean every reading is within 10 cm?

No. With AmIMo anchors, AmIHub places a tag down to ±10 cm in 3D. How close depends on your floor, so you measure it on yours in the pilot.

Do AmIMo anchors need data cables?

No data or sync cable. Each AmIMo anchor syncs over the air and sends its data to AmIHub over Wi-Fi. Ethernet is also an option. Each one needs power, from DC or PoE.

Does AmIMotion replace our MES?

No. Your MES stays in charge of every job. AmIHub adds where its trolley is and how long it has waited, over open interfaces.
