One tag. Every carrier. Every vehicle. Located.

Tags ride on what moves. Anchors locate them. AmIHub turns every position into a place and a time, and answers your systems and your AI.

AmIMotion is a complete ultra-wideband (UWB) real-time location system from Bengaluru: AmIGo tags, AmIMo anchors and AmIHub software. It locates tagged trolleys, bins, tools and vehicles down to ±10 cm in 3D (with AmIMo anchors), and records which zone each one is in and for how long.

Where and how anchors mount.
  • Mount on a column, wall or ceiling, 3 to 8 m up.
  • Set it up from a phone app. AmIHub finds each anchor itself.
  • Each anchor reaches about 50 m in line of sight. Steel and racking set the final count.

Down to ±10 cm. Right down to the shelf.

Down to ±10 cm in 3D, with AmIMo anchors. Height counts, so the system knows the rack level, not only the aisle. Your site sets the final figure.

How a pulse becomes a place.
  • Each tag sends a pulse a few nanoseconds wide. Anchors time its arrival to tens of picoseconds.
  • Anchors also read the angle a pulse arrives from, so fewer anchors fix a position.
  • The wide band separates the direct signal from reflections off steel and racking.
  • Your site sets the final accuracy. A site survey sets the anchor plan.

How it locates a tag

How a position is made. In under a second.

A tag sends a very short radio pulse. Anchors time it and read where it came from. AmIHub works out the spot.

  1. The tag speaks

    AmIGo sends a pulse a few billionths of a second long, whenever it moves.

  2. Anchors listen

    Each AmIMo in range times its arrival and reads the angle it came from.

  3. AmIHub solves

    Time and angle become a 3D position on the live map.

  4. Your systems hear

    Zone entries and exits reach your MES, ERP or WMS over open interfaces.

Questions teams ask first.

Does ±10 cm mean every reading is within 10 cm?

No. Down to ±10 cm in 3D is the figure with AmIMo anchors. Your pilot measures it on your floor, in the hard spots as well as the easy ones.

Is AmIMotion open or proprietary?

Open. AmIMotion is built on the FiRa and omlox specifications, and AmIHub speaks REST, MQTT and WebSocket and has an omlox hub interface. Your data is not tied to one vendor.

Do the anchors need cables?

No data or sync cables. AmIMo anchors sync over the air and send data over Wi-Fi, or Ethernet if you prefer. Each one needs power, from DC or PoE.

Can AI assistants use the data?

AmIHub has a built-in MCP server, so MCP-capable AI assistants can query live positions, zones and history. A person approves any action. Shown in a live demo.
