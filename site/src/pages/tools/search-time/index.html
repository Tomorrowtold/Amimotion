What searching costs you.

Put a number on the time your team spends looking, from your own figures.

Searching for trolleys, bins, tools and forklifts costs hours every shift. This calculator multiplies your own figures, people, searches and minutes, into hours a year. AmIMotion’s tags put each item on a live map, located down to ±10 cm in 3D, so a search starts on a screen. The calculator assumes no savings.
