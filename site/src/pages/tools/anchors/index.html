How many anchors? A first range.

Your floor in, a first anchor range out, before anyone visits. A site survey sets the real number.

AmIMotion plans the final anchor count from a site survey. This estimator gives a first budget range from floor size and racking, using planning grids of about 25 m for an open hall, 18 m with some racking and 12 m with dense high racking. An AmIMo anchor reaches about 50 m in line of sight (indicative, site-dependent).
