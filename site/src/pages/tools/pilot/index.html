Your pilot on one page.

Three answers become a brief you can send to your plant head, IT and finance. It is written in your browser.

AmIMotion's pilot planner turns three answers into a one-page brief. You choose what you need to understand, where you would begin and how work is linked to trolleys today. The brief lists what to settle before hardware arrives, such as the zones that matter and who acts when work waits too long. Copy it, download it as a text file or open it as an email. Nothing leaves your browser until you send it.

Who the brief is for.

A pilot needs more than one yes.

  • Your plant head

    What the AmIMotion pilot asks, where it runs and what you decide at the end.

  • IT and OT

    Where the anchors go and what they need. Each AmIMo anchor needs only power, and AmIHub can run on an industrial PC inside your plant, with no internet needed.

  • Finance and procurement

    What is in scope, so the prices in the NDA pack can be read against it.

What the brief asks you to settle.

  • The zones that matter, named in your own words.
  • Who on your team acts when work waits too long.
  • How to count a wait that runs past shift end.
  • A baseline, measured before you compare anything.
  • How a job is linked to its trolley, in AmIHub or from your own system, if you need job-level waits.

Until a job is linked to its trolley, AmIHub shows a trolley's wait, not a job's wait.
