Plan it before the call.

Free tools that run in your browser. No sign-up.

AmIMotion offers four free planning tools: a floor diagnostic, a UWB anchor estimator, a search-time calculator and a pilot planner. Each runs in the browser and nothing is stored.

  • How many anchors you need Your floor size in, a first anchor range out, before a site survey.
  • Time spent searching Put a number on the time your team spends looking.
  • Your pilot on one page Describe one bay. Get a pilot brief to bring to the call.
