Plan it before the call.

Four free tools. They run in your browser, ask for no sign-up and send nothing unless you choose to.

AmIMotion offers four free planning tools. Plan your first bay asks six questions and gives back a plan for where to start. How many anchors you need gives a first range from your floor size, before a survey. Time spent searching turns your own figures into hours a year. Your pilot on one page writes a brief to share with your team. Each one runs in your browser.

  • Time spent searching

  • Your pilot on one page

    How do I explain it to my team? A brief to share.

What these tools are not.

They are planning aids, built from your answers and a few stated assumptions. None of them is a quote, a survey or a forecast of savings. Each AmIMotion tool shows its assumptions next to its answer.

The real anchor count comes from an AmIMotion survey of your floor. The real change in search time comes from a pilot that measures it, against a baseline set before it starts.

Nothing you type is stored by this site. A brief you download is a file on your own device. If you email a result, it goes from your own mail app.
