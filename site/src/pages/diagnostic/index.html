Plan your first bay.

Six questions about how work moves in your plant. You get a plan for where to start, before anyone calls you.

AmIMotion's floor plan asks six questions: what your team looks for, how they find it today, where work waits longest, how a job is linked to its trolley, where you would start, and your role. It sketches your floor as you answer. At the end you get a plan for one bay or line: what to tag first, which zones to name and who should see the live map. Your answers stay in this browser tab until you choose to send them.

What the plan is, and is not.

The plan is a starting point built from six answers. It is not a survey, a quote or a promise about your floor. Racking and steel change how far each AmIMo anchor reaches, so we survey your floor and set the count.

You can print the plan or save it as a PDF. Start over clears your answers.

If you choose to send it, the page opens a draft email to AmIMotion in your own mail app, with your plan, your name and your work email. Nothing reaches us until you press send. We use it only to reply about your floor. Privacy
