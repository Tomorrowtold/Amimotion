What should a pilot measure first? The baseline, before any tag goes on.

What to count, how to count it fairly, and what you can claim after.

Before claiming that RTLS cut search time, measure a baseline on the same flow: how often people search, how long each search takes, how far they walk, how long carriers wait in each zone and how many records need correcting. Measure it before any AmIGo tag goes on, then measure the same things with AmIMotion's live map, where positions reach down to ±10 cm in 3D, depending on the site. A fair comparison covers normal variation, such as two weeks of shifts, and reports the spread, not the best day.

What to measure

  • Searches per shift, and minutes per search.
  • Walking distance or time to find a carrier.
  • Time in key zones, measured by hand or from existing scans.
  • Corrections to records, such as wrong locations or statuses.

Once the AmIMotion tags are on, time in zones comes from AmIHub. Searches, walks and corrections are counted the same way as before, so before and after compare like with like.

How to measure fairly

Use the same flow, shifts and product mix before and after. Let the people who search record it, on a simple sheet. Two weeks is better than two days. Agree the measures with your team before the AmIMotion pilot starts.

What you can then claim

Compare like with like and report the spread, not just the best day. A financial case needs your real costs on top, and your team owns those numbers. In an AmIMotion pilot you see your own waits in one bay, and prove the numbers, before you decide on more.

A head start

The search-time calculator turns your own figures into hours a year, as a starting point for the baseline. The calculator assumes no savings, and AmIMotion never quotes one from a calculator.

Common follow-ups.

What should be measured before claiming RTLS reduced search time?

Searches per shift, minutes per search, walking time, zone waits and record corrections, on the same flow, before tags go on.

How long should a baseline run?

Long enough to cover normal variation. Two weeks is a sensible minimum.

Will AmIMotion calculate the financial case?

Not from a calculator. A sound case needs a measured baseline and your real costs.
