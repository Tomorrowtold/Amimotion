Measure before
you claim.

The short answer

Before claiming that RTLS reduced search time, measure a baseline on the same flow: how often people search, how long each search takes, how far they walk, how long carriers wait in each zone and how many records need correcting. AmIMotion pilots measure this before tags go on, then compare with the live map, where tags are located down to ±10 cm in 3D.

What to measure

  • Searches per shift, and minutes per search.
  • Walking distance or time to find a carrier.
  • Time in key zones, measured by hand or from existing scans.
  • Corrections to records, such as wrong locations or statuses.

How to measure fairly

Use the same flow, shifts and product mix before and after. Let the people who search record it, with a simple sheet. Two weeks is better than two days.

What you can then claim

Compare like with like and report the spread, not just the best day. A financial case needs real costs on top, which your team owns.

A head start

The search-time calculator turns your own numbers into hours and cost per year, as a starting point for the baseline.

Common follow-ups.

What should be measured before claiming RTLS reduced search time?

Searches per shift, minutes per search, walking time, zone waits and record corrections, on the same flow, before tags go on.

How long should a baseline run?

Long enough to cover normal variation. Two weeks is a sensible minimum.

Will AmIMotion calculate the financial case?

Not from a calculator. A sound case needs a measured baseline and your real costs.
