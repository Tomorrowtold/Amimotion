Every figure carries its condition. AmIMo anchors locate AmIGo tags down to ±10 cm in 3D, depending on the site.

Four quick answers.

  • Is UWB accurate enough for trolleys?

    Usually, when anchors are planned for the floor. AmIMotion quotes down to ±10 cm in 3D with AmIMo anchors, typical and site-dependent. How to test it.

  • UWB, BLE or RFID?

    RFID and scans prove a trolley passed a point. BLE finds the zone. UWB, which AmIMotion uses, tells neighbouring queues apart. Choose by the question.

  • How many anchors does a floor need?

    A survey decides. As a first budget, an open 5,000 m² hall on this site's 25 m planning grid needs about 15 AmIMo anchors. What sets the count.

  • Does a tag know the job on the trolley?

    No. AmIMotion links each trolley to its job in AmIHub or from your own system. Why the link matters.
