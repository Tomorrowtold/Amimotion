What is
WIP visibility?

The short answer

WIP (work-in-progress) visibility means knowing where each job physically is and how long it has waited at each step. WIP tracking only shows where a carrier is; visibility adds which job it holds and its time in each zone. AmIMotion delivers it with AmIGo tags on carriers, AmIMo anchors that locate them down to ±10 cm in 3D, and AmIHub, which links each carrier to its job and times every zone.

Three things it needs

A tag on the carrier. The trolley, bin or rack carries its own tag.

A reliable position. Anchors locate the tag and every reading carries its time. UWB can tell neighbouring queues apart.

A link to the job. A time-limited link between trolley and job, from your MES, ERP or a scan.

What it tells you

Where a job is, where it has been, when it entered each zone and how long it waited. With an agreed time limit, a long wait becomes a flag for a named person.

What it cannot tell you

Being in a zone does not prove work started or finished. A long wait does not explain why. Location never authorises an action by itself.

How to start

Pick one flow where people walk to find work today. Tag its trolleys, agree a few zones and one time limit, and name who acts. Measure how work is found before and after.

Common follow-ups.

What is the difference between WIP visibility and WIP tracking?

Tracking shows where a carrier is. Visibility adds which job it holds and how long it waited in each zone.
