What is
omlox?

The short answer

omlox is an open standard for interoperable industrial location systems. It defines a common data model and hub interface, so location data from different technologies and vendors, such as UWB, BLE and RFID, can feed one location hub. AmIMotion is built on the omlox specification: AmIHub offers an omlox hub interface next to REST, MQTT and WebSocket, and its UWB positions are accurate down to ±10 cm in 3D (with AmIMo anchors).

Why it exists

Plants often end up with several location systems: RFID at the docks, BLE for people, UWB for carriers, a fleet manager for robots. omlox lets them meet in one hub instead of one integration per vendor.

The parts

omlox covers a hub that collects location data and a standard way for devices and systems to talk to it. Applications read one model of zones, objects and events, whatever technology produced them.

Compliant, or certification?

Compliant means a company says its product follows a specification. A formal certification means an authorised body has tested it. AmIMotion describes itself as built on the FiRa and omlox specifications, and that is the claim to read.

omlox in AmIMotion

AmIHub has an omlox hub interface, and its data and device model follow the FiRa and omlox specifications. That keeps your location data open, whichever systems you add later.
