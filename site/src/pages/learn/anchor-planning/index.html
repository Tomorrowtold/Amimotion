How many UWB anchors
do I need?

The short answer

There is no honest formula from floor area alone. The anchor count depends on the accuracy you need, 2D or 3D, the positioning method, ceiling height and how much racking and metal block line of sight. An AmIMo anchor reaches about 50 m in line of sight (indicative, site-dependent), and its angle of arrival lets as few as two anchors in view fix a position. As a first budget only, an open 5,000 m² hall planned on a 25 m grid needs around 15 anchors; dense racking can double that. AmIMotion sets the final count from a site survey.

What sets the count

The task. “Which hall?” needs far fewer anchors than “which of two adjacent lanes?”.

The method. With TDoA a tag must be heard by several anchors at once: at least three for 2D and four for 3D. With two-way ranging plus angle of arrival, fewer are needed.

Line of sight. Tall racking, machine guards, mezzanines and parked vehicles block the direct path. Aisles between high racks often need anchors that see down each aisle.

Geometry. Anchors in a straight line give poor accuracy across that line. Spread them around the area the tag moves in.

Mounting and power. AmIMo anchors go on columns, walls and ceiling structure, 3 to 8 m up, and need only DC or PoE power.

A worked example

Take an open hall of 100 × 50 m (5,000 m²) with clear sight lines. Planned on a 25 m grid, that is 5 × 3 = 15 anchor positions. With mixed racking, a tighter grid of around 18 m gives about 28. Dense high racking can need a grid of 12 m or less.

These grids are this site’s planning assumptions for a first budget, not an AmIMotion specification. Try your own floor in the anchor estimator.

How to get to a number

  1. Mark the areas where location changes a decision. Leave the rest out of scope.
  2. Write the accuracy and update rate each area needs.
  3. Note ceiling height, racking, aisles and large metal objects.
  4. Choose a positioning method per area.
  5. Lay out anchors for sight lines and geometry, then confirm with a survey and a short pilot in the hardest spot.

Questions to ask any vendor

Is the anchor count from a survey of my floor or a rule of thumb? What accuracy is it designed for, and where will it be worse? What cabling does each anchor need? How is accuracy measured at acceptance?

Common follow-ups.

Can I calculate UWB anchors from floor area?

Only roughly. Area ignores racking, ceiling height, metal and the accuracy you need. Use area for a first budget, then a site survey for the plan.

How far does a UWB anchor reach?

AmIMo reaches about 50 m in line of sight, indicative and site-dependent. Obstructions reduce the useful range.

Do UWB anchors need network cables?

Not AmIMo anchors: they sync over the air and send data over Wi-Fi, with an Ethernet option. Each still needs power.
