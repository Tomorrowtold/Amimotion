Accessibility statement.

Everyone who runs a floor should be able to read this site, with any device and any way of using it.

The AmIMotion website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. Every page is tested with automated checks and by keyboard before it ships. Moving scenes have still pictures and text that say the same thing, motion stops when your device asks for less of it, and sound never starts on its own. If something gets in your way, write to sales@amimotion.com and we will fix it or send you the content another way.

What we aim for

The site aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. That covers text contrast, keyboard use, visible focus, headings and labels that screen readers can follow, text that can be enlarged, and pages that work on a phone held either way.

How we test it

Before a change ships, every page is checked with axe-core against the WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A and AA rules, and the main paths are walked by keyboard alone. The last full check was on 16 November 2026.

Motion, sound and 3D

When your device is set to reduce motion, the scroll scenes and 3D views stay still and show a picture instead. The words on each scene are real text on the page, so screen readers and search tools read them. Sound plays only after you choose to turn it on, and a switch turns it off.

Known limits

The 3D views and the live AmIHub example are pictures for the eye. Their content is also written out in words on the same page. The films carry no speech; if you need a description of one, ask and we will send it.

Tell us

If any part of this site is hard to use, write to sales@amimotion.com. Say which page and what happened.
