AmIMotion starts small on purpose. One flow, in one bay or line, measured on your own floor before anyone talks about more.

AmIMotion pilots start in one bay or one line. You choose the flow. The pilot times today's waits first, so you have a baseline. You see your own waits in one bay, and prove the numbers, before you decide on more. Then you grow plant-wide on the same AmIHub, without re-fitting the first bay.

What a first bay proves.

A pilot follows one flow in one bay or line. AmIGo tags ride on the trolleys, bins or pallets in that flow. AmIMo anchors on the bay's columns hear them. AmIHub shows where each one is and how long it has waited.

Before it starts, we agree how to count a wait that runs past shift end. We also agree who on your team acts when work waits too long.

When the bay has shown its numbers, AmIMotion grows plant-wide on the same AmIHub. The first bay is not re-fitted.
