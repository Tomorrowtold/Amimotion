Specifications. Every figure, with its condition.

These are AmIMotion's published figures. Each one carries what it depends on. The final datasheets come under NDA after the first call.

AmIMotion is a UWB location system in three parts. AmIGo tags use IEEE 802.15.4z HRP radio on channels 5 and 9 and report 0.1 to 10 times a second. AmIMo anchors time each pulse, read its 3D angle of arrival and need only DC or PoE power. AmIHub places tags down to ±10 cm in 3D with AmIMo anchors, depending on your floor, and runs inside your plant or in the cloud. AmIMotion is built on the FiRa and omlox specifications.

Questions about the figures.

Which positioning methods does AmIMotion use?

Several, chosen per zone in AmIHub: UL-TDoA, DL-TDoA, two-way ranging and 3D angle of arrival. Most asset tracking uses UL-TDoA, where AmIGo tags send and AmIMo anchors time the pulse.

What update rate do AmIGo tags support?

0.1 to 10 Hz, set per tag group. AmIGo tags sleep when still and send when they move, which is why one battery lasts 1 to 3 years, typical.

How accurate is AmIMotion?

Down to ±10 cm in 3D, typical, with AmIMo anchors. It depends on your floor, so you measure it on yours in the pilot, near racking and machines too.

Why are size and weight not listed?

AmIMotion shares them in the final datasheet, under NDA after the first call, with the hardware revision you would evaluate.

Does AmIMotion hold a formal certification?

AmIMotion is built on the FiRa and omlox specifications. It claims no formal certification.
