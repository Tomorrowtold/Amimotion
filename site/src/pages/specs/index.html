AmIMotion is an ultra-wideband (UWB) location system built on IEEE 802.15.4z HRP and on the FiRa and omlox specifications. AmIGo tags report 0.1 to 10 times a second, AmIMo anchors combine time difference of arrival with 3D angle of arrival, and AmIHub locates tags down to ±10 cm in 3D, on-premise or in the cloud.

Questions teams ask first.

Which positioning scheme does AmIMotion use?

Several, chosen per zone: UL-TDoA, DL-TDoA, two-way ranging and 3D angle of arrival. Most asset tracking uses UL-TDoA, where tags send and anchors time the pulse.

What update rate do AmIGo tags support?

0.1 to 10 Hz, set per tag group. Tags sleep when still and send when they move, which is why one battery lasts 1 to 3 years, typical.

How accurate is AmIMotion?

Down to ±10 cm in 3D with AmIMo anchors. The figure depends on the site, so a pilot measures it on your floor, including near racking and machines.

Does AmIMotion hold FiRa or omlox certification?

It is built on the FiRa and omlox specifications. It does not claim certification. AmIHub has an omlox hub interface for mixing vendors.
