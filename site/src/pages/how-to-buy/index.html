How to buy.

Call, NDA pack, survey, pilot.

What you buy. AmIGo tags, AmIMo anchors and AmIHub, plus our survey and an anchor plan for your floor. Designed and built in India, with local stock and local engineers on your site. One contract and one support line cover every layer.

Four steps.

  1. Call

    The call is a live demo on real AmIHub data, not slides.

  2. NDA pack

    After the first call, we share datasheets, architecture, prices and pilot options under NDA.

  3. Survey

    We survey your floor and set the anchor count.

  4. Pilot

    A pilot follows one flow in one bay or line. You see your own waits in one bay, and prove the numbers, before you decide on more.

Your team or your integrator can build on it at any layer, through open interfaces. AmIMotion is led by Kaushal Kukkar, founder and CEO, and Vivek Dham, CTO.
