How to buy. Four steps, in order.

AmIMotion sells one system, from one team, under one contract. Here is what you buy and how it reaches your floor.

You buy AmIGo tags, AmIMo anchors and AmIHub from AmIMotion, plus our survey and an anchor plan for your floor. It happens in four steps. First, a call with a live demo on real AmIHub data. Second, the NDA pack with datasheets, architecture, prices and pilot options. Third, a site survey and a pilot in one bay or line. Fourth, growth plant-wide on the same AmIHub. One contract and one support line cover every layer.

Four steps.

  4. Grow

    Then grow plant-wide on the same AmIHub, without re-fitting the first bay.

What you buy.

Three parts, and the work to fit them to your floor.

  • AmIGo tag

    One on each trolley, bin, pallet or tool in scope. One battery lasts 1 to 3 years, depending on use.

  • AmIMo anchor

    Fixed to a column or wall, 3 to 8 m up. It needs only power, from DC or PoE.

  • AmIHub

    Works out where each tag is. Runs on an industrial PC inside your plant, with no internet needed, or in the cloud.

  • Survey and plan

    An AmIMotion survey of your floor and an anchor plan for it, so the anchor count fits your racking and steel.

What procurement asks.

  • Who is the supplier?

    AmIMotion is a brand of Locatepro Technologies Pvt Ltd, Bengaluru, India. It is led by Kaushal Kukkar, founder and CEO, and Vivek Dham, CTO.

  • Who supports it?

    One contract and one support line cover every layer. AmIMotion designs and builds the tags, the anchors and AmIHub in India, with local stock and local engineers on your site.

  • Are we tied to one supplier?

    AmIMotion is built on the FiRa and omlox specifications. Your MES, ERP and WMS connect to AmIHub through open REST, MQTT, WebSocket and omlox hub interfaces. Your team or your integrator can build on it at any layer.

  • What will the quote cover?

    Prices are in the NDA pack, after the first call. If you need hardware, services and software on separate lines, say so on the call.
