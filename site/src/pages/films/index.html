Films. One floor, in motion.

Short films of one model factory floor, seen from above. Each one shows a single idea, with no sound and nothing to read.

AmIMotion's films show one model factory floor from above. Blue lines show where tagged trolleys, tools and vehicles went. Amber marks the trolleys and pallets that are waiting. The first film runs nine seconds: the same floor before and after AmIMotion, from scan ticks at three desks to every path and wait on one live map.

What you are looking at.

  • Blue

    Where a tagged thing went. An AmIGo tag sends a short UWB pulse as it moves, and AmIMo anchors on the columns hear it.

  • Amber

    Where work waits. AmIHub notes when each trolley or pallet enters a zone and when it leaves, so you see a wait while it is still a wait.

  • Pale

    Everything nobody has tagged.

The nine seconds, in words.

The film opens on the floor as AmIMotion shows it. Blue trails run behind every moving thing: the tugger's train, the forklift, an AMR, a trolley leaving the press. Three trolleys in the inspection queue and one pallet in the staging lane stand on amber.

At two seconds the colour goes. This is the floor as most plants see it. At three checkpoint desks, a short blue tick appears as a trolley passes. Nothing marks the path between desks. One trolley passes a desk, turns behind the racks and stops. No mark follows it.

Then the ticks join into lines, one kind of thing at a time: trolleys, bins and a fixture, the tool cart, the forklift and tugger, the AMR. The three trolleys and the pallet take their amber again. At nine seconds the frame is back where it began.
