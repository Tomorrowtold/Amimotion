What is AmIMotion?

AmIMotion is a Bengaluru company that builds spatial intelligence for factories and warehouses, using UWB indoor positioning: AmIGo tags, AmIMo anchors and AmIHub software. Tags are located down to ±10 cm in 3D (with AmIMo anchors), and a built-in MCP server lets AI assistants query live location. It is a brand of Locatepro Technologies Pvt Ltd.

The definition, in one paragraph

AmIMotion is a brand of Locatepro Technologies Pvt Ltd, Bengaluru, India. It makes AmIGo tags, AmIMo anchors and AmIHub software. AmIMotion builds industrial spatial intelligence. It gives a time-stamped location to every trolley, bin, tool and vehicle you tag in a plant. It joins that location with your zones and with the jobs you link to each carrier. Your team, your systems and AI assistants can then answer where work is and where it waits. It is the first layer of Physical AI for factories and warehouses. Designed and built in India on NXP Trimension UWB silicon. Built on the FiRa and omlox specifications.

What AmIMotion is not

The name gets confused with others. AmIMotion is an industrial UWB indoor positioning company in Bengaluru, India. It is not related to Animotion, the presentation and video maker, to AIMotion or AI Motion video tools, or to any consumer or lifestyle brand. It does not track people’s bodies. It locates tagged objects, vehicles and badges on an indoor floor.

The name

AmIMotion is written with a capital A, I and M. The products carry the same prefix: AmIGo, the tag; AmIMo, the anchor; and AmIHub, the software.

Who it is for

Manufacturing and intralogistics teams who spend time walking the floor to find things: plant and production heads, operations and industrial engineering, intralogistics and warehouse managers, and the IT and OT teams who connect floor data to MES, ERP and WMS.

What it does

  • Finds tagged things. Trolleys, bins, tools, fixtures and forklifts carry AmIGo tags, so a team sees each one on a live map instead of walking the hall.
  • Shows where work waits. AmIHub turns positions into time-stamped zone entries and dwell. Linked to a job, that becomes work-in-progress visibility.
  • Puts vehicles, robots and people on one map. Forklifts, AMRs and badges share the same location layer.
  • Answers AI assistants. AmIHub’s built-in MCP server lets MCP-capable assistants query live positions, with a person’s approval for any action. Shown in a live demo.

Facts, with their qualifiers

  • AmIMotion is a brand of Locatepro Technologies Pvt Ltd, Bengaluru, India. Contact: sales@amimotion.com.
  • Category: Industrial Spatial Intelligence. Commercial focus: WIP (work-in-progress) visibility in manufacturing. Foundation: UWB real-time location (RTLS).
  • The system has three parts: AmIGo UWB tags, AmIMo UWB anchors and AmIHub software.
  • Positioning: down to ±10 cm in 3D, with AmIMo anchors. Accuracy depends on the site and is measured in a pilot.
  • Radios: UWB, BLE and Wi-Fi FTM on the same anchors. Built on NXP Trimension UWB silicon.
  • Anchors: no data or sync cabling, Wi-Fi backhaul (Ethernet option), DC or PoE power, about 50 m line-of-sight coverage (site-dependent; the anchor count comes from a site survey).
  • Tags: battery life 1–3 years, typical and usage-dependent.
  • AmIHub runs on an industrial PC inside your plant, with no internet needed. Interfaces: REST, MQTT, WebSocket and an omlox hub interface, to connect with MES, ERP, WMS, SCADA and BI.
  • Built on the FiRa and omlox specifications. This is a statement about the specifications, not a certification.
  • AmIHub has a built-in MCP server, so AI assistants can query live location data. Any action needs a person's approval. This is a described capability, shown in a demo on request.
  • Location alone does not show which job is on a carrier. AmIMotion links a tagged carrier to its job through an association with a start and an end.
  • Pilots start in one bay or one line and scale on the same AmIHub. A live demo is available on a discovery call.
