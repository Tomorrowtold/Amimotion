The short answer

Industrial spatial intelligence is precise, time-stamped location of everything that moves in a plant, such as carriers, pallets, tools, vehicles and robots, joined with jobs and zones, so people, systems and AI assistants can answer where work is and where it waits. AmIMotion builds it for factories and warehouses, on UWB positioning down to ±10 cm in 3D. It is the first layer of Physical AI, built and supported in India.

Questions teams ask first.

What is Industrial Spatial Intelligence?

Precise, time-stamped location of what moves in a plant, joined with context such as jobs and zones, so people, systems and AI can answer where work is and where it waits.

How is it different from RTLS?

RTLS is the location layer. Spatial intelligence adds the context and the answers on top of it.

Where does AmIMotion start?

With WIP visibility in factories: tagged carriers, linked to jobs, timed in every zone. In warehouses the same records time pallets and totes from dock to stock.
