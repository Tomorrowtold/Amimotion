What is spatial intelligence?

Industrial spatial intelligence is a time-stamped location for every trolley, bin, tool and vehicle you tag in a factory or warehouse, joined with your zones and with the jobs each carrier holds. With it, people, plant systems and AI assistants can answer where work is and where it waits. AmIMotion builds it, on UWB positioning down to ±10 cm in 3D with AmIMo anchors, depending on the floor.

Work in progress·How it works

Three layers. One answer.

Location on its own is a dot on a map. Spatial intelligence stacks what the dot means on top of where it is.

  1. Where each tagged thing is, down to centimetres, and the time it was seen. AmIMotion gets it from UWB: AmIGo tags and AmIMo anchors.

  2. The zones you name and the job each carrier holds. A position becomes: this job's trolley is waiting at inspection, and this is how long.

  3. Supervisors read it on AmIHub's live map. Your MES and ERP read it over open interfaces. AI assistants ask it over MCP.

How it differs. From a scan, and from RTLS.

Three ways to know about the same trolley over the same ninety minutes.

What you knowWhen you know itThe question it answers
A barcode or RFID scanWhere a thing was when someone or something scanned itAt each checkpoint“Did it pass this point?”
RTLSWhere a tagged thing is nowAll the time, as it moves“Where is it?”
Spatial intelligenceWhere it is, which zone it is in, which job it holds, and for how longAll the time, with time stamps“Where is work waiting, and for how long?”

Each fits a different job. Where a record at each checkpoint is all you need, a scan is enough. AmIMotion is for the gaps between checkpoints.

Where AmIMotion starts. One bay. Then the plant.

AmIMotion starts with work in progress in one bay of a factory. Tagged trolleys and bins, linked to their jobs, are timed in every zone you name. You see your own waits in that bay before you decide on more.

The same AmIHub then grows plant-wide without re-fitting the first bay. In a warehouse it times pallets and totes from dock to stock. Forklifts, tuggers and AGVs carry vehicle tags on the same anchors, so work and vehicles share one map.

Questions about the category.

What is industrial spatial intelligence?

It is the time-stamped location of what moves in a plant, joined with your zones and jobs, so people, systems and AI assistants can answer where work is and where it waits. AmIMotion builds it for factories and warehouses.

How is spatial intelligence different from RTLS?

RTLS is the location layer: where a tagged thing is. Spatial intelligence adds the zones and the jobs, so the answer is where work waits and for how long. AmIMotion's AmIHub does both.

Does spatial intelligence need AI?

No. A supervisor reads AmIHub's live map. AI assistants are one more way to ask it, through AmIHub's MCP server, and any change needs a person's approval.

Where does AmIMotion start?

With work in progress in one bay: tagged carriers, linked to their jobs, timed in every zone. Then plant-wide on the same AmIHub.
