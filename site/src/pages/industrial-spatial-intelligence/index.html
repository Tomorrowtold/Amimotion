Industrial Spatial Intelligence.

Industrial spatial intelligence is precise, time-stamped location of everything that moves in a plant, such as carriers, pallets, tools, vehicles and robots, joined with jobs and zones, so people, systems and AI assistants can answer where work is and where it waits. AmIMotion builds it for factories and warehouses, on UWB positioning down to ±10 cm in 3D. It is built and supported in India.

How it works

Three layers. One answer.

  • Where, precisely

    UWB positions to centimetres, each with the time it was seen.

  • What it means

    Zones, jobs and rules: which job is on the trolley in the inspection queue, and how long it has waited.

  • Who can ask

    Supervisors on a map, MES and ERP over open interfaces, and AI assistants over MCP.

  • Work in progress Which job waits where, and for how long.

Questions teams ask first.

What is Industrial Spatial Intelligence?

Precise, time-stamped location of what moves in a plant, joined with context such as jobs and zones, so people, systems and AI can answer where work is and where it waits.

How is it different from RTLS?

RTLS is the location layer. Spatial intelligence adds the context and the answers on top of it.

Where does AmIMotion start?

With WIP visibility in factories: tagged carriers, linked to jobs, timed in every zone. In warehouses the same records time pallets and totes from dock to stock.
