AmIMotion, answered plainly.

AmIMotion is a brand of Locatepro Technologies Pvt Ltd, Bengaluru, India. It makes AmIGo tags, AmIMo anchors and AmIHub software. AmIMotion builds industrial spatial intelligence. It gives a time-stamped location to every trolley, bin, tool and vehicle you tag in a plant. It joins that location with your zones and with the jobs you link to each carrier. Your team, your systems and AI assistants can then answer where work is and where it waits. It is the first layer of Physical AI for factories and warehouses. Designed and built in India on NXP Trimension UWB silicon. Built on the FiRa and omlox specifications.

How it works.

How accurate is AmIMotion?

Down to ±10 cm in 3D, with AmIMo anchors. Accuracy depends on the site, so a pilot measures it on your floor.

Do the anchors need cables?

No data or sync cabling. Anchors sync over the air and send data over Wi-Fi, with an Ethernet option. Each anchor needs power, from DC or PoE.

Does UWB work in a factory full of steel and racking?

UWB’s wide bandwidth separates the direct signal from reflections. AmIMotion adds non-line-of-sight algorithms and plans anchor density per zone.

How long do tag batteries last?

1 to 3 years, typical and usage-dependent. Tags sleep when still and send when they move.

Does AmIMotion track individual parts?

It tags the carrier: the trolley, bin or rack. Which parts or job it holds comes from a carrier-to-job link, not from the radio.

Systems, data and AI.

Can it run without the internet?

Yes. AmIHub can run on an industrial PC inside your plant with no internet dependency, or in the cloud.

Does it connect to our MES or ERP?

AmIHub offers REST, MQTT and WebSocket and an omlox hub interface, and can push events to MES, ERP, WMS, SCADA or BI. Connectors are confirmed for each site.

Does AmIMotion hold FiRa or omlox certification?

AmIMotion is built on the FiRa and omlox specifications. It claims no formal certification.

Does AmIMotion have an MCP server for AI assistants?

Yes. AmIHub has a built-in MCP server, so MCP-capable AI assistants can query live positions, zones and history. Actions need a person’s approval, and every call is logged. Shown in a live demo.
