AmIMotion

AmIMotion, a brand of Locatepro Technologies Pvt Ltd in Bengaluru, India, makes a complete UWB real-time location system: AmIGo tags, AmIMo anchors and AmIHub software. Manufacturers use it to see where work in progress is and where it waits. Designed and built in India on NXP Trimension UWB silicon. Built on the FiRa and omlox specifications.
