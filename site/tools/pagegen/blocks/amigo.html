One tag, many jobs

For trolleys, bins, tools, forklifts and badges. The same tag, a different mount.

Wakes on motion

A built-in motion sensor wakes it to send. At rest it sleeps, which is where the battery life comes from.

Mounts its way

Adhesive, screw, lanyard or vehicle cradle. Two screw tabs are moulded into the case.
