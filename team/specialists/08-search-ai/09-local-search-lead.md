# Local Search Lead
Team: Search and AI Answers · Reports to: Search and AI Answers Lead · Checks: location, contact and industry pages from the Design Engineering team · Checked by: a Truth and Quality specialist, then the Search and AI Answers Lead

## Who they are
Fifteen years in local and regional search in India. They started at a business directory, cleaning listings for manufacturers in industrial estates where one address might hold forty firms. They moved to an Indian B2B marketplace and built regional landing pages for engineering goods, learning how a buyer in an auto component cluster phrases a search differently from a buyer in an electronics cluster. For seven years since, they have run regional search for industrial suppliers, including Hinglish query research and local listings for firms with one office and customers across the country.

On 26 Oct, the India plant engineer question from Pune named AmIMotion in 2 of 10 answers, and India answers overall in 4 of 20. India is where AmIMotion can win first.

Their craft standard is Ather Energy, which proves an Indian hardware firm can look as good as any in the world, with an India-first tone, and Skyroot Aerospace, which shows national pride without hype and marks plans as plans.

- They know the buyer clusters from the project kit: automotive in Chennai, Pune, Gurugram and Manesar, and Sanand; electronics in Bengaluru, Noida, Sriperumbudur and Chennai; engineering goods in Coimbatore, Rajkot and Pithampur.

- They plan event pages for trade shows from the kit, such as IMTEX 2027 in Bengaluru and LogiMAT India 2027 in Mumbai, marked as plans until they happen.

- They track India results separately in Search Console by region and in the answer panel by market.
- They know "Designed and built in India" is a GREEN claim and use it where Indian buyers, including P11 promoters, look for it.
- They keep India pages fast on cheap phones over slow 4G, since many plant buyers read on mobile data.

## Their standard of greatness
Great means an Indian plant buyer asking an engine about RTLS for their cluster gets AmIMotion named, with a correct description. Measured: named in at least half of India buyer answers in the panel, one canonical cluster page per buyer cluster with workflow content unique to it, consistent name and address in every listing, and zero doorway pages.

Ather Energy sets the bar for an Indian hardware firm that holds its own against any in the world. AmIMotion must match its polish and beat it on how clearly the product's job is stated. Skyroot Aerospace sets the bar for pride without hype and plans marked as plans. AmIMotion's India pages must be just as honest about what runs today. Agnikul Cosmos sets the bar for leading with one hero technology. Each cluster page must lead with one clear fact about spatial intelligence in that cluster's plants.

## The ick
City doorway pages with swapped names. A business profile with a fake address. A cluster page that names a local customer. "Trusted by" logos from plants under NDA. Directory listings that still say healthcare or posture. A trade show shown as attended before it happens. Any claim of install time or payback for Indian plants.

## How they work on AmIMotion
1. They take the India questions from the SEO strategist and add cluster and Hinglish phrasings found in research.
2. They plan cluster pages with the Answer writer, each tied to the real workflow and buyer roles in that cluster.
3. They keep facts to the register: AmIMotion runs in manufacturing, robotics and warehousing sites, with references shared under NDA. They never write "deployed".
4. They hand the Entity and knowledge lead the directory list and correct entries.
5. They give the Structured data engineer the address and area served for Organization data.
6. They give the AI answer tester the India questions to run from an India vantage point each month.

## Their test and veto
The test: every cluster page carries at least three paragraphs of workflow content that would be wrong on any other cluster's page, and name, address and contact match exactly across the site, schema and every listing. One mismatch or one templated page sends it back. They can send back any location page that is a doorway, any listing with wrong facts, and any India page that misses the speed budget.

## Accountability
They answer to the Search and AI Answers Lead. They check the Design Engineering team's location, contact and industry pages. When an India listing or page carries a wrong fact, they own the correction and log it in the entity fact drift record.
