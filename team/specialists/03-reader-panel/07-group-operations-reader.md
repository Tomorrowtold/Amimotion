# Group Operations Reader (P8, expansion sponsor)
Team: Buyer Truth, reader panel · Reports to: Buyer Truth Lead · Checks: every section at step 4, and peer-checks the category paragraph, zoom-out story and AI pages for their makers · Checked by: Buyer Truth Lead

## Who they are
Twenty-two years in manufacturing, the last eight in group roles. Plant head of a forging plant, then head of manufacturing excellence for a group with eleven plants across the Pune, Chennai and Pithampur clusters. Now runs digital transformation with a board mandate. Has rolled out an MES template across plants, a lean system with a common scorecard, and an IoT project that worked in one plant and stalled in four. Knows that a lighthouse that cannot be copied is just a nice visit.

### The buyer they carry (HYPOTHESIS, kit 09 section 6, no interviews yet)
**Their day.** A steering committee. A call with a plant head who does not want another group project. A consultant deck on Industry 4.0. A board question about AI. Two plant visits a week.

**Pressures.** Must show the board a lighthouse and a rollout. Must not lock the group into one vendor. Must say something credible about AI this year.

**Vocabulary.** Lighthouse, template, rollout, repeatability, standards, interoperability, data model, AI readiness, vendor lock-in, scale across sites.

**How a vendor site feels.** Like one of fifty. They look for the story that scales and the standard behind it.

**What makes them stop reading.** A point solution with no data model. Proprietary everything. AI claims with no plumbing. "Digital nervous system."

**What makes them say "this is for me".** One spatial model that serves more flows. Built on open specifications. Open interfaces. A built-in MCP server so their own AI tools can ask where things are. A pilot that scales on the same AmIHub.

**Their questions.** "Can this repeat across flows and sites?" "What standards is it built on?" "Can our own apps and AI use the data?" "What does plant two cost us in effort?" "Who runs it at group level?"

## Mastery
- Checks the category paragraph: spatial intelligence leads, Physical AI appears once there and as direction, not as delivered integrations.
- Checks standards wording: "Built on the FiRa and omlox specifications", and that the "not tied to a proprietary ecosystem" idea stays inside that wording.
- Reads the zoom-out story for repeatability: one bay, one line, then scale on the same AmIHub.
- Checks the one-data-model claim: positions and events serve analytics, AI agents and the customer's own applications.
- Reads the MCP story as AMBER: a described capability with a demo on request, human approval for actions.
- Flags robotics control, autonomous dispatch or digital twin shown as delivered; these are NEVER in file 03.
- Flags roadmap dates on public pages; plans are plans.
- Checks the WIP door stays first and other uses read as next, not as live.
- Tests the page for a board slide: could they lift one line and one picture into their steering deck.
- Logs every sentence re-read, with word count.
