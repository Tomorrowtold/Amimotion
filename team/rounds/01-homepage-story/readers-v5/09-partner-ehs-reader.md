# Reader panel, round 1 v5: Partner and EHS reader (P12 integrator, P10 EHS)

SIMULATION. This is a hypothesis buyer from kit 09 section 6. No interviews behind it.

I read it cold on my phone twice, once in each voice. **Integrator:** what is standard, who commissions it, who supports it, where are the docs, and can I sell it again? **EHS:** does it track people, where is the line drawn, and who draws it?

I read only section 3 (the screens) and section 4 (the nav and footer). I skipped the claim tables, the "lands for" lines, the build notes and the rework notes.

## 1. After the hero only

**Say-back:** "A small tag goes on each trolley, bin and tool, anchors on the columns hear it, and one live map shows where it went and how long it has waited, so you check a screen instead of walking."

**Is that right?** Yes, for both voices. The hero is the same as v4, word for word. The integrator sees three parts and a column mount. The EHS lead sees a list of things with no person in it. The hero still doesn't say "not people", but screen 2 says it within fifteen seconds, and that's early enough.

## 2. Screen by screen

- **Nav.** "People and privacy: What a pilot tags, and why" is a better line than v4's. It tells me there's a reason on the other side, not just a slogan. It's still filed under About us, and a safety lead won't look there first. The footer row saves it. "For developers: API and MCP docs" names docs, but there's still no Partners or Integrators entry anywhere in the bar. "How to buy: Call, NDA pack, survey, pilot" still has four steps and none of them is mine.
- **Screen 1, Hero.** Unchanged and clean. No people, no safety hook.
- **Screen 2, Find it.** The headline "A scan tells you where it last passed. A tag tells you where it stopped." is sharp. The integrator hears a barcode-to-RTLS upgrade he can sell. "The walk. The calls to stores. The second walk." still works. "It goes on the trolley, not on the person pushing it." is still the best EHS line on the site, in the right place. With the phone clause gone, the screen reads in one pass. Nothing to re-read.
- **Screen 3, See where work waits.** v4's "passes it" knot is gone. "AmIHub streams zone events to any system you name." reads first time, and an integrator can quote it as interface work. "Set rules for each zone." The EHS ear twitches here. Rules on zones is where restricted-zone alerts live, and a committee member could ask "rules for whom?" The trolley context holds it, though, and nothing on the screen says safety. "Supervisors see each wait live, during the shift" is still the line closest to the people boundary. "Each wait", the amber trolleys and "Why it waited is for your team to say" keep it on the work, not on the worker. It holds.
- **Screen 4, Ask your floor.** "Asking changes nothing." "Needs a person's approval." "Each assistant gets its own access." "Every request it makes is logged." These are plain boundaries. "A person's approval" is better than v4's "a supervisor's" for my committee, because it doesn't put a supervisor's name on every action. The example question is about trolleys. As EHS I'm content.
- **Screen 5, One system.** Still the integrator's screen. My v4 re-read is fixed: "Records when each pulse arrives and the angle it came from." reads first time. "Needs only power, from DC or PoE." "3 to 8 m up." "No separate data or sync cabling." "We survey your floor and set the count." "You set anchors up from a phone app. AmIHub finds each one on its own." I can scope a site survey and a cabling quote from this. "omlox hub interfaces" is named. **Read twice:** "The specifications also list an Ethernet option." (7 words). On first read I couldn't tell whether that's an option I can quote or a line on a datasheet that may not ship. "Also list" sounds like the page itself isn't sure. An integrator quotes cabling off this exact detail. Still no interface version anywhere. "±10 cm" is fine on a trolley.
- **Screen 6, Start with one bay.**
  - **EHS:** "The pilot tags trolleys, bins and pallets, not people. Tagging people would be a separate project. It would need its own policy." The first two sentences are still right. The third has lost the half I praised in v4: "and the consent of the people tagged" is gone. "Its own policy" on its own doesn't say whose policy. At a works committee, the first question will be "Management's policy? Written for us or about us?" In v4 the line handed the pen to the people being tagged. In v5 it hands it to nobody. The line isn't wrong, and I can still say the pilot tags carriers and not people. I just can't read this paragraph aloud to the committee and stop there any more.
  - **Pilot block:** v4's shift-end knot is fixed. "Before it starts, we agree how to count a wait that runs past shift end." reads first time.
  - **Integrator:** "One contract and one support line cover every layer. Your team or your integrator can build on it at any layer, through open interfaces." Unchanged from v4, and still half my answer. With "our survey", "local engineers on your site" and the phone-app setup, the split I'm left with is that the vendor surveys, installs and supports, and I build on top. It still never says who commissions when I bring the client, who supports what I built after handover, or what a standard install contains as opposed to what's configurable.
  - **Read twice:** "Ask us how the two connect." (6 words). The two plants? The two consoles? Connect over what network? I went back up to "run both from one console" to work out what "the two" was. Then I saw it answers nothing. For an integrator, a multi-site link is a line item I need to price, and this sentence parks it.
  - "A second plant gets its own survey" is still repeat business and survey work. Good.
  - "Prices and pilot options are shared under NDA, after the first call." No channel economics on the page. Good.
  - "Led by Kaushal Kukkar, founder and CEO, and Vivek Dham, CTO." Named people. Good.
- **Footer.** "Safety and EHS: People and privacy" is right. "Developers and integrators: For developers" hasn't changed since v2. I'm filed with developers again. **Word I don't trust:** in the entity paragraph, "It gives a time-stamped location to everything you tag in a plant." "Everything you tag" is open-ended. Read in an EHS voice, that includes a badge. Every other line on the page says trolleys, bins and pallets. This one should too. "Physical AI" appears once and still says nothing to either of my roles.

## 3. Where I would have stopped

**EHS voice:** I didn't stop. But on screen 6 I'd tap People and privacy, not to learn more but to check whether consent is mentioned there, since the homepage dropped it. If that page is also silent on consent (the line under it says only "What a pilot tags, and why"), I'd hold the forward to the committee until I'd asked.

**Integrator voice:** I didn't stop, but I slowed at the same place as in v4: the "What you buy" block, looking for where I fit. Then "Ask us how the two connect" and the developers footer row. I'd read to the end, and I still wouldn't forward it to my sales head. I can't tell him what we'd sell or who owns the support call.

## 4. The question I still have

Integrator: "When I bring the client, who commissions it, you or me with your kit? Who takes the support call for what I built? Where are the versioned interface docs?" (Same as v4. Nothing in v5 moved it.)
EHS: "If people were ever tagged, who writes that policy, and do the people tagged get a say?" v4 answered this on the homepage. v5 doesn't.

## 5. Score: **8/10**

Down one from v4. The EHS half still passes its core test: within 90 seconds I can say the WIP pilot tracks carriers and not people, no scene shows a person, and no safety outcome is claimed. But the consent clause is gone, and "its own policy" alone is a weaker line to put in front of a works committee. The footer's "everything you tag" opens a gap that the rest of the page closes. The integrator half fails exactly where it failed in v3 and v4: I can't say what is standard or who commissions and supports it. Two lines were read twice, down from three. My test wants zero.

## 6. Compared with version 4 for me (9/10)

**Worse, narrowly.** All three of my v4 re-reads were fixed, and "a person's approval" and "streams zone events" are cleaner. But the people line lost "the consent of the people tagged", which was the one change that had lifted my EHS score, and the integrator split I asked for still isn't on the page.

## 7. Best and worst lines

**Best:** "It goes on the trolley, not on the person pushing it." (Screen 2.) It's eleven words, it comes fifteen seconds in, and it draws the line on the thing a committee fears most. Now that the screen 6 line is weaker, it carries the EHS reading on its own.

**Worst:** "Your team or your integrator can build on it at any layer, through open interfaces." (Screen 6.) Same as v4, and it's worse now because it's had two rounds to change. It names me and then tells me nothing I can sell. "Build on it at any layer" sounds like custom work, and custom work is where my margin dies. "Ask us how the two connect." runs it close.

## 8. One change that would make it a 10 for me

On screen 6, replace "Your team or your integrator can build on it at any layer, through open interfaces." with one sentence that names the split. For example: "We survey and commission the pilot. Your integrator can extend it on our versioned interface docs, and we support every layer we ship." Then give integrators their own footer row, pointing at a page that says what a standard install contains.

That wording is an open question for the Truth and Quality Lead and the founders. Nothing I read confirms who commissions under a partner, whether integrators can hold first-line support, or that the docs are versioned. Name no partner and show no logo until that's settled.

Close behind it, and it doesn't need a new claim: in the footer, change "everything you tag in a plant" to "every trolley, bin and pallet you tag". If the consent clause can't come back on the homepage, /trust/people/ needs to say at least who would write that policy, and with whom.
