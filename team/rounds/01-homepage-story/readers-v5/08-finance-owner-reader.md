## 7. Best and worst line

**Best:** "One contract and one support line cover every layer." (screen 6). The whole start-up worry answered in nine words, and it is a promise procurement can write into a contract.

**Worst:** "Ask us how the two connect." (screen 6). Six words that tell the multi-plant promoter it is unsolved and tell procurement there is an unpriced item. Cut it; "AmIHub can run both from one console" already stops at the right place.

## 8. One change that would make it a 10

Name installation, the AmIHub PC and what recurs in "What you buy", with no figures, and put a founder on the call:

> **What you buy.** AmIGo tags, AmIMo anchors and AmIHub, plus our survey, an anchor plan, installation and [the AmIHub PC, or "a PC you supply to our spec"]. After the pilot, what recurs is [AmIHub licence or none, support, batteries]. ...
>
> **See it on real data.** The call is a live demo on real AmIHub data, not slides, with [a founder or our CTO] on it.

Then procurement has every BOQ line named before the NDA, and the promoter's "can I meet the founders?" is answered yes before he taps. Both bracketed items need founder confirmation; if the founders cannot keep the call promise every time, do not print it.

## Flags for the Buyer Truth Lead and the Truth and Quality Lead

- **Installation not named** in "What you buy". Survey and anchor plan are. Hidden-scope risk, third round.
- **AmIHub PC.** "Runs on a PC inside your plant." Supplied or customer-bought? Not in "What you buy". Hidden-scope risk, third round.
- **Recurring costs unnamed.** Battery life and level shown; who replaces, and whether AmIHub or support recurs, is not.
- **"Ask us how the two connect."** Makes no claim, but reads as an unscoped, unpriced item to procurement and as "not solved" to a two-plant promoter. Recommend cut.
- **Founder line moved off the call.** Correct per V95 (cannot read as who joins a call). Commercial cost: the promoter's strongest trust trigger from v4 is gone. If the founders do join first calls, a sourced line in the call block would restore it.
- **"Link a trolley to each job it carries"** reads as ongoing work per job. Who does it, and is it in pilot scope? /pilot/ or /how-to-buy/ should say.
- **No stop line.** B46 STOP holds. Gap, not error.
- **"Time spent searching" tool.** Confirm no saving, payback or ROI figure (NEVER in file 03).
- **"First layer of Physical AI"** in the footer. Slogan among facts. Not RED; noted.
- **No "who else uses it" signal.** Deployment line still cut (STOP). Gap, not error.
- **No RED found.** No funding, revenue, traction count, investor name, customer name, logo, testimonial, ROI, payback, saving figure, or exact pilot price, duration or deployment day.
