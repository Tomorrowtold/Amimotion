  - Small loss: "on a screen or on your phone" is gone. My supervisors live on their phones. Now "Look it up on AmIHub's live map instead" makes me picture a PC in an office, which is the walk again by another name.
  - "AmIHub" first shows up here as a name with no introduction. The hero said "one live map". I worked it out, but it is one more word to carry.
- **Screen 3, "See where work waits."** The v4 line I read twice ("When a trolley passes it...") is gone. Good. "Supervisors see each wait live, during the shift, not at the next morning's review." still hits.   - Won: "The pilot times today's waits first, over a window you choose. That is your baseline." Still the line I repeat.
