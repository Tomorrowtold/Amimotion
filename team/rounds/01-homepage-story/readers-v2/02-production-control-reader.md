Better, but only a little. Waiting now has a screen of its own, the association line exists ("You link each tagged carrier to its job"), "last seen" is said honestly, and MCP is explained and set aside for IT.

## 7. Best and worst lines

- **Best:** "You see how long each one has waited, while it is still waiting." (Screen 3). Close second: "The walk. The calls to stores. The second walk." (Screen 2).
- **Worst:** "A long wait shows you where to look." (Screen 3). It sits where the alert should be, it names no one, and it puts the looking back on me. Runner-up: "A scan tells you where it was. A tag tells you where it is." (Screen 2), because the page itself says a parked trolley shows where it was last seen.

## 8. One change that would make it a 10 for me

On screen 3, replace "A long wait shows you where to look." with the step that is missing, in the same plain voice, and back it with one real AmIHub capture: **one trolley passes its limit at inspection and the wait lands with a named role.** For example: "Set a limit for each zone. When a trolley passes it, the person you choose is told: Last seen at Inspection at 10:42. Waiting 52 min, limit 30." Put the association step on the same screen, in one sentence: "Link the job card to the trolley once, when it leaves the cell. After that, the job's wait is the trolley's wait." If AmIHub cannot send that alert today, say so plainly and say who looks at the map instead. A named person looking is still better than "shows you where to look". Then I can say in one breath: "It finds my trolleys and tells the right person what is waiting before it is late." I would forward that today.

### Flags for the makers (production-control reasons)
- No owner, no limit, no alert anywhere on the page. Screen 3 and screen 4 ("A person approves any action") both stop before a named person acts.
- Stale-data wording: screen 5 passes ("where it was last seen"). The hero ("shows where it is") and the screen 2 headline ("A tag tells you where it is") contradict it for a sleeping tag.
- Association: told straight only in the footer, and as one line with no "how" on screen 5. The footer row "Which job is on the trolley?" should also be offered to production and planning, not only to MES owners.
- Screen 2 fallback: if it ships without the real capture, the page has no finding moment.
- Barcodes and RFID are never said to fail. Good. "Everything you tag" in the footer is right. Good.
