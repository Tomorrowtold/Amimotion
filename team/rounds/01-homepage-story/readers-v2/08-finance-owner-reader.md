# Reader 08: Finance and Owner (P9 procurement, P11 promoter). Round 1, homepage v2

SIMULATION. I am not a real buyer. I am a chartered accountant who has sat beside a promoter for nine years. I am reading on a phone between a bank call and a budget review. I read in two voices: **Procurement** ("what am I buying, what recurs, who supports it") and **Promoter** ("who are these people, will they come when it breaks").

Read: section 3 copy and picture descriptions, section 4 nav and footer. Nothing else.

---

## 1. After the hero only

"AmIMotion sells a small tag for each trolley, bin and tool, anchors that go on my columns, and a live map that shows where each thing is and how long it has waited."

Right. This is better than any round 1 hero for me. Concept B's hero left the anchors out. This one puts both pieces of hardware and the software in five sentences, so I could write a rough BOQ heading from the hero alone. The first words, "spatial intelligence for factories and warehouses", are a category name, not an answer, but by the second sentence I know what I am buying. "You find out from a screen, not a walk." is still in the hero. It is the closest thing to a saving claim on the page. I accept it as a method line, but I noticed it.

## 2. Screen by screen

- **Nav.** Procurement: "Start small" is the best nav word for a cautious approver, and it is still there. But there is no "How to buy" anywhere, in the nav or the footer. Pricing, contract and payment terms have no home. Promoter: "About us" is where he will look for faces. Fine.
  - Did not trust: "Time spent searching / Your own numbers". It is still a calculator that works out my losses for me. If it prints a rupee figure or a payback, I close the tab.
- **Screen 1, Hero.** Procurement: three parts named. Good. Promoter: a clean model hall with no people. It is honest, because it is clearly a model and not a borrowed stock photo. But nothing in it is Indian, and nothing in it is them.
- **Screen 2, Find it.** Procurement: no line items, and that is fine. It is the supervisor's screen. Promoter: "The walk. The calls to stores. The second walk." He has done that walk on a plant visit and been angry about it. It lands. A real, unedited AmIHub recording earns more trust from me than any mock-up.
- **Screen 3, See where work waits.** Procurement: nothing for me. Promoter: "Why it waited stays your team's call." He trusts a vendor who says what it does not do. Short, calm, done.
- **Screen 4, Ask your floor.** Promoter: the board's AI question gets a plain answer, and "A person approves any action" answers the fear before he can say it. "We show it live on a call" is honest: it tells me the AI part is a demo, not a promise. Procurement: "every request it makes is logged" covers the obligations question.
  - The small IT block is no longer confusing, because it now says "AmIHub has an MCP server built in" before it says "the server". Round 1's re-read is fixed. I still skip the block. It is not for me, and it is set smaller, so that is fine.
  - Did not know: "MCP" (now explained in one line, good enough).
- **Screen 5, One system.** Procurement: this is still the line-item screen, and it is clear. A tag, an anchor and AmIHub, each with one job. "One battery lasts 1 to 3 years, depending on use." So batteries recur. Who replaces them, and at whose cost? "Anchors need only power... No data or sync cables to run." No cabling scope is hidden. Good. "We survey your floor and plan the anchors." So the survey is a service.
  - Read twice: "Works out where each tag is, down to ±10 cm with AmIMo anchors. Your site sets the final figure." (18 words). To a finance person, "final figure" means a price. I read it as "your site sets the final price" and had to go back. It means accuracy. Say "Your site sets the final accuracy."
  - Did not trust: "±10 cm" (whose test?), "Your MES stays in charge" (a commercial obligation, I want it in the contract, not just on the homepage).
  - Did not know: "PoE", "FiRa", "omlox". Only one spec line now, not the five-name line from round 1. Better.
- **Screen 6, Start with one bay.** Procurement: "**What you buy.** AmIGo tags, AmIMo anchors and AmIHub, plus our survey of your floor." This is the block I asked for in round 1, and it is mostly right. "One team owns every layer, so you have one contract and one support line." It answers two of my four questions in one sentence. "You get a plan for where to start, before anyone calls you." This is the Formlabs feeling: something I can hold before a salesman phones. That is the opposite of a sales trap.
  - Missing: setup, installation and support are not named as parts of what I buy. Only the survey is. So I still do not know if installing the anchors is in the price or a separate line.
  - Missing: no price line. After "What you buy" I expect to read how pricing works. Nothing is there. "Book a call" is again "contact us for pricing" with nothing else.
  - Missing: no stop. "Then grow plant-wide on the same AmIHub." comes straight after "You choose the flow you want to see." Round 1 B's "you can stop at any of them" is gone. Promoter: this is where he slows down. A pilot that only grows is a commitment, not a small step.
  - Missing: no face. "See it on real data" tells me what we see on the call, not who we see.
  - Promoter: "Designed and built in India" is now in a bold block he will actually reach. It is a strength here, not a footnote. Good. "Running more than one plant? Ask us how the second one starts." He runs two plants. He likes being asked.
- **Screen 7, Footer.** Procurement: "Procurement and finance: How a pilot runs." In round 1 B, it was "How to buy". This is a step back: I am sent to the pilot page, not a buying page. Promoter: "Owners and MDs: About us." He keeps his own row. Good.
  - The only NDA sentence on the page is "Final datasheets are shared under NDA." It is about datasheets, not prices, and it sits in the trademark small print.
  - Did not trust: "It is the first layer of Physical AI for factories and warehouses." It is a slogan in a paragraph that is otherwise plain facts.
  - Good: "AmIMotion is a brand of Locatepro Technologies Pvt Ltd, Bengaluru, India." A real company name and city. This is the first answer to "who is behind this company". I would look up the company record that afternoon.

## 3. Where I would have stopped

Procurement would not stop. Screens 1, 5 and 6 are what I came for, and they are where I expected them. The promoter would skim 2 to 5 and stop at screen 6, at "Book a call". He has read "Designed and built in India" and "one team", but he still has not seen one person, and there is no line that says he can walk away. He would not book. He would forward it to me and ask "find out who these people are".

## 4. The question I still have

Procurement: "What do I pay for the survey and the setup, and what do I pay every year after the pilot (AMC, batteries, AmIHub)?" Promoter: "Who are the founders, and will one of them be on this call?"

## 5. Score

**7 / 10.** Procurement can name what is bought and who supports it within the 90 seconds. The promoter cannot name why to trust these people, the pilot has no exit, and there is no price line.

## 6. Compared with the best of round 1 for me (Concept B, 7)

**Same.** It is better for procurement: the anchors are now in the hero, there is a real "What you buy" block, and "Designed and built in India" is where the promoter will see it. It is worse for the promoter: the stop line is cut, the footer lost "How to buy", and the three empty slots (stop, price, face) are exactly the three things I asked for.

## 7. Best and worst line

**Best:** "One team owns every layer, so you have one contract and one support line." (screen 6). It is my two procurement questions answered in fourteen words, placed in the block I will actually read.

**Worst:** "Then grow plant-wide on the same AmIHub." (screen 6). With the stop line cut, it is the only line about what happens after the pilot, and it only goes one way. A cautious approver reads it as "the pilot is the first instalment". (Close second: "Your site sets the final figure.", which a money reader takes as a price.)

## 8. One change that would make it a 10

Finish the "What you buy" block on screen 6, so it covers the three empty slots in one place:

> **What you buy.** AmIGo tags, AmIMo anchors and AmIHub, plus our survey, setup and support on your floor. Designed and built in India. One team owns every layer, so you have one contract and one support line. Prices are shared under NDA, after the survey. Each stage of the pilot has a check before the next, and you can stop at any of them.
>
> On the call: [name], who built it. [photo; public details or written approval only]

This names deployment as its own part. It turns "Book a call" from a hidden quote request into an honest next step. It gives the cautious approver his exit. It gives the promoter a face. All three depend on founder answers (items 1, 11, 12), so they must be settled first. Until then this page stays at 7.

## Flags for the Buyer Truth Lead and the Truth and Quality Lead

- **No pricing line.** The reserved slot is empty. Without it, "Book a call" reads as "contact us for pricing" with nothing else. Item 11.
- **No stop option.** "Each stage has a check before the next, and you can stop at any of them." was cut by STOP. "Then grow plant-wide" now stands alone. Item 1. If the founders cannot confirm a gated pilot, at least say the pilot is one bay and ends with a review.
- **No face.** The pass test ("the promoter asks to meet the founders") fails again. Item 12. Bios are AMBER: public details or written approval only.
- **Deployment services not named in full.** "Survey" is a part. Setup, installation and support are not. Procurement cannot tell whether installation is a separate line.
- **Footer regression.** Procurement now goes to "How a pilot runs", not "How to buy". I accept the reason (no pricing wording yet), but it should come back the day item 11 is answered.
- **"Your site sets the final figure."** Reads as price to a finance reader. Suggest "final accuracy".
- **"Time spent searching" tool.** Confirm it shows no saving, payback or ROI figure (NEVER in file 03). The note says the Lead Magnet Lead checks this. I want to see the output before launch.
- **"Your MES stays in charge."** It is a commercial obligation. Confirm it with the founders before it ships.
- **Deployment line cut.** "Runs in automotive, robotics and warehousing plants." is gone (STOP). The page now has no "who else uses it" signal at all. The promoter asks that question second. That is a gap, not an error.
- **No RED found.** No funding, revenue, traction count, investor name, customer name, logo or testimonial.
