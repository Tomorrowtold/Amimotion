# Reader panel, round 1, v2: Group Operations Reader (P8, expansion sponsor)

**SIMULATION.** This is a hypothesis buyer from kit 09 section 6, read by the panel. No real interviews sit behind it.

I read it on my phone, in the car between a plant visit and the steering committee, the same way as last time. I read only section 3 (screens) and section 4 (nav and footer). I am looking for three things: the category in one line, a story that repeats across my eleven plants, and an AI line I can take to the board without it blowing up on me.

---

## 1. After the hero only
Say-back: "AmIMotion is spatial intelligence for factories and warehouses: a tag on what moves, anchors on the columns, one live map of where it is and how long it has waited."

Is that right? Yes. The category comes first, Physical AI is not in the opening, and the three parts are named without product names. On a phone the poster is the "after" frame and sits halfway down the first screen, so I get the picture without waiting for the film. The one line I skip is "You find out from a screen, not a walk." It is written for my plant head. I could not repeat it to my board, but it does no harm.

## 2. Screen by screen
- **Nav.** "How it works · Where it helps · Start small · About us". Buyer words, and "Start small" still tells me the pilot is bounded. No menu entry is for the group buyer. "Your pilot on one page: To share inside your plant." Singular again.
- **Hero.** Calm and certain. I could lift the poster into a steering deck as the "why" slide.
- **Screen 2, "A scan tells you where it was. A tag tells you where it is."** The board line survived, and the plainer form is fine. It respects the MES we already paid for. The rest ("The walk. The calls to stores. The second walk.") is for my supervisors, so I skim it. To me this screen is a point solution until I see the model behind it.
- **Screen 3, See where work waits.** "You name the zones" is the right answer to my template question: the zones belong to us. The picture puts the inspection queue and the staging lane in one frame, factory and stores on one map, and that is half my repeat test. "Why it waited stays your team's call" is honest, and it sells no alert it does not have.
- **Screen 4, Ask your floor.** This is the screen I was worried about, and it is now clean. "Any assistant that supports MCP can connect. We show it live on a call." That is the right wording, with the AMBER qualifier right beside it, which is what I asked for. "A person approves any action" and "every request it makes is logged" give my IT head nothing to fight. Read twice: "The server runs where AmIHub runs, including on a PC inside your plant." (14 words). I have not been told where AmIHub runs yet, because screen 5 tells me. It still sends me forward to find the answer.
- **Screen 5, "Three parts work as one system."** This is the headline I asked for: one system, not one team. It passes my Anduril test, since tag, anchor and hub are presented as one thing. "Open REST, MQTT and WebSocket interfaces connect it to your MES, ERP and WMS" and "Built on the FiRa and omlox specifications" make the no-lock-in case without ever saying "lock-in". "You link each tagged carrier to its job" and "Your site sets the final figure" are honest edges, and I trust a vendor more for them. What I miss is the line from round-1 C: "people, your systems and AI assistants read the same floor." The one-data-model sentence is no longer anywhere in the scroll. It survives only in the footer paragraph.
- **Screen 6, Start with one bay.** "Then grow plant-wide on the same AmIHub." That is still the plant wall. Then, last on the screen, under the button block: "Running more than one plant? Ask us how the second one starts." It is honest, it claims nothing it cannot back, and it is the first time any version has admitted that my question exists. But it sits in the final block, where I am deciding whether to book, not in the pilot story where the scale question belongs. "One team owns every layer, so you have one contract and one support line." I can live with that, because the open interfaces are on screen 5. Without them it would read as lock-in. The "Runs in automotive, robotics and warehousing plants" line is gone. Good.
- **Footer.** "Group operations and digital: How it works." That is better than the definition page I got last time, because it goes to the system and not to a dictionary. But it is still a product page, not a page about rollout. The entity paragraph no longer says the same thing twice, and Physical AI appears once, as direction ("the first layer of"). That passes. "Your team, your systems and AI assistants can then answer where work is and where it waits." That is my one-data-model line, at the bottom of the page where I will not see it on a phone.

Words I did not trust: none. The things I distrusted in round 1 ("Runs in", "tells the room", "the assistant your team already uses", "Partners") are all gone.

## 3. Where I would stop
I did not stop. I slowed at screen 2, which is the plant head's screen, and leaned in at screens 4 and 5.

## 4. My question at the end
"Plant two: same AmIHub, same zones, and how much effort from my team, with who running it at group level?" The page now invites the question ("Ask us how the second one starts") but still does not answer it.

## 5. Score: 8 / 10
It is the first version with no veto line in it. Physical AI does not lead, nothing implies lock-in, no plan is shown as live, the MCP wording is exact, and the system screen is headlined about the system. I would forward it to a plant head today as a candidate lighthouse. It is not a 9 or a 10, because the repeat story still stops at the plant wall and the one-data-model sentence has dropped out of the scroll.

## 7. Best and worst lines
- **Best:** "A scan tells you where it was. A tag tells you where it is." (screen 2). It is the board slide line. A close second: "Three parts work as one system." (screen 5), which is the headline for that slide.
- **Worst:** "The server runs where AmIHub runs, including on a PC inside your plant." (screen 4). It defines one unknown by another, and I read it twice. Right wording: "The MCP server is part of AmIHub, so it can run on a PC inside your plant."

## 8. One change that would make it a 10
Put the one-data-model sentence back on the scroll, on screen 5 straight after the interfaces line, in the footer's already-passed wording (A65): "Your team, your systems and AI assistants read the same live map." Then move "Running more than one plant? Ask us how the second one starts." up next to "Then grow plant-wide on the same AmIHub." That puts the scale question in the pilot story, and the footer row for group buyers can point to it. **Truth flag:** a real answer to plant two (multi-site on one AmIHub, reusable zone sets, group-level running) still needs a founders' source. Until then the question is the right form, and it must not become a capability claim.
