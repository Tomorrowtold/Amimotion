# Reader panel, round 1 v2: P4 Supervisor Reader

SIMULATION. This is a hypothesis buyer from kit 09 section 6. No real supervisor was interviewed. I am reading as a line-side logistics supervisor at a Tier 1 plant. It is the end of a twelve-hour shift and I am on a mid-range phone with a cracked screen. I read sections 3 and 4 of homepage v2 only.

My questions going in: Will it save me the walk? Does it track me or the trolley? Who sees the alert first? Does it work on my phone? What if the tag falls off? Do I have to scan anything?

---

## 1. After the hero only

"A small tag goes on each trolley, bin and tool, boxes on the columns hear it, and I look at a map instead of walking to find it."

That is right. "You find out from a screen, not a walk." is still there, and it is still my whole day in one line. The first sentence still says "spatial intelligence". I still do not know that word, but now I skip it and the next four sentences carry me. Five short sentences. I read the hero once.

## 2. Screen by screen

- **Nav.** "How it works, Where it helps, Start small, About us." I can read all four. On my phone it is a menu with "Plan your first bay" first. Fine. I would not open it.
- **Hero.** Short, plain, trolley first. The film has no people. Good. On my phone it crops to the inspection queue and the racks, so I see trolleys standing on amber. That I understand: those are the ones waiting. The "before" with the checkpoint desks still shows scanning, not walking. My problem is the walk, and nobody walks in the film. But the copy says it, so I let it go.
- **Screen 2, "A scan tells you where it was. A tag tells you where it is."** Plainer than last time. I got it the first time. "Think of the last time someone went looking for a trolley. The walk. The calls to stores. The second walk." Still the truest lines on the page. "The tag reports its own position, so nobody scans it along the way." Good. That answers "do I have to scan". The picture is now a real AmIHub capture. That is what I asked for. But it says "If AmIHub runs in a phone browser, record it on a phone. If not, record the desktop." So I still do not know if it works on my phone. And until the capture exists, the fallback is a render with no screen at all. **Word I did not trust:** "Look it up on the map." Which map? On what? Say phone, if it is true.
- **Screen 3, "See where work waits."** "Take the trolleys at inspection or the pallets in a staging lane." My words. "A long wait shows you where to look. Why it waited stays your team's call." Still the best truth line. It does not blame my operators. **Read twice:** "Each move into and out of a zone gets a time." (11 words.) "Gets a time" is odd. I had to think: a time stamp? I got it the second time. The picture, three trolleys on amber, one being worked on, I understood in one look.
- **Screen 4, "Ask your floor a question."** "Which trolleys have waited at inspection for more than an hour?" A trolley question. Good. "A person approves any action." Good. The MCP part is now set small and says it is for IT. I skip it, and that is the right place for it. "Each assistant gets its own access, and every request it makes is logged." Now I can see it logs the assistant, not me. Last time "every call is logged" scared me. This is fixed. **Word I did not know:** MCP. But I am told I can skip it.
- **Screen 5, "Three parts work as one system."** I read the AmIGo block only, as the page says. "Screws or sticks onto the trolley." "One battery lasts 1 to 3 years, depending on use." "While it sleeps, the map shows where it was last seen." That is three of my day-one questions answered in four sentences. "Last seen" I trust. I glanced at the other two blocks. **Words I did not know:** PoE, ±10 cm, REST, MQTT, WebSocket, FiRa, omlox. **Read twice:** "Works out where each tag is, down to ±10 cm with AmIMo anchors. Your site sets the final figure." "Your site sets the final figure" means what? That my plant decides? I got it the second time: it depends on our floor. Say that.
- **Screen 6, "Start with one bay."** **Read twice, and it made me stop for a second:** "A pilot watches one flow in one bay or line." (10 words.) "Watches" is the word my crew is afraid of. Two sentences later: "A pilot tags carriers, not people." That is the line I asked for. It is here now, and it fixes the fear. But "carriers" is an office word. On the floor we say trolley. And it sits on screen 6, where my crew will never scroll. **Read twice:** "On the call we show the live map and an AI question on real AmIHub data, not slides." (18 words, the longest I read today.) "Then grow plant-wide on the same AmIHub." Not for me. The line "you can stop at any of them" is gone. I liked that one. Systems leave our plant with no goodbye; that line told me this one would not be forced on us.
- **Footer.** "Shift supervisors: Where is that trolley?" That is my question, in my words, as the link to my page. Best footer of all of them. The long paragraph at the bottom: "everything you tag in a plant", not "everything that moves". Fixed. "Your team" not "People". Fixed. **Words I did not trust:** "first layer of Physical AI", "NXP Trimension UWB silicon". Consultant English. But it is at the very bottom and I would not read it.

## 3. Where I would stop

I would not stop before screen 5. Screens 1 to 4 are short enough to read at the end of a shift. On screen 5 I would read the tag block and stop, at "Open REST, MQTT and WebSocket interfaces", because that is IT's block, not mine. I would scroll past to the footer only to find my link.

## 4. The question I still have

Who gets told first when a trolley waits too long, me or my manager? The page now avoids promising any alert at all, so it never answers. And: does the search open on my phone, or only on the office PC?

## 5. Score: 7/10

It sees my walk, answers the tag, battery and scanning questions, and no line hints at watching people except "a pilot watches". It still does not promise my phone, and the "not people" line is too far down and in office words.

## 6. Compared with the best of round 1 (B, 6/10)

Better. The two things that scared me are fixed: "everything that moves" is now "everything you tag", and "logged" now clearly means the assistant's requests, and "a pilot tags carriers, not people" is finally said.
It is not a 10 because the phone is still a maybe ("if AmIHub runs in a phone browser"), the "you can stop" line was cut, and nobody says who gets the alert first.

## 7. Best and worst lines

- **Best:** "You find out from a screen, not a walk." (Screen 1, hero.) Still the line I would read aloud to my crew.
- **Worst:** "A pilot watches one flow in one bay or line." (Screen 6.) "Watches" next to my floor is the one word my crew hears as surveillance. Say "A pilot follows one flow" or "A pilot tracks the trolleys in one flow". Runner-up, for reading load: "Open REST, MQTT and WebSocket interfaces connect it to your MES, ERP and WMS." (Screen 5.)

## 8. One change that would make it a 10

Make screen 2 a real AmIHub search on a phone, at phone size, a trolley found in seconds with "last seen" showing, and put one plain line under it: "We tag the trolley, not you." That moves the promise from screen 6 into the screen I actually read, in my words, and shows me it works on my phone. If it does not run on a phone yet, tell me that plainly instead of showing a desktop crop.

Still open for the founders: who gets the wait alert first. If it reaches my manager before me, I am against it from day one.
