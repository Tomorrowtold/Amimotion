# Reader panel, round 1: Partner and EHS reader (P12 integrator, P10 EHS)

SIMULATION. This is a hypothesis buyer from kit 09 section 6. No interviews behind it.

I read each concept twice on my phone, once in each voice. **Integrator voice:** I commissioned conveyors, vision and an RTLS pilot. I want to know what I can sell again, what is standard, who commissions it, who supports it and where the docs are. **EHS voice:** I run a Tier 1 plant safety team and sit across from a works committee. I want to know whether it tracks people and where the line is drawn.

I read only section 3 (the screens) and section 4 (the nav).

---

## Concept A: "Where work is. Where it waits." (scan gap, camera A)

### 1. After the hero only
**Say-back:** "Small tags go on the things that move. Anchors on the columns pick them up. A live map shows where each thing is and how long it has sat still."
**Is that right?** Yes, for what the product is. The integrator voice gets the three parts from one paragraph. The EHS voice notices "No people" in the film and relaxes a little. Nothing on the page actually says so, though. Leaving people out of a picture is not the same as drawing a boundary.

### 2. Screen by screen
- **Nav.** Integrator: Platform has "For developers", good. There is no "Partners" anywhere. EHS: there is no safety or privacy entry anywhere. The closest is "Trust and data" under Company. I don't trust the word "Uses": it is vague.
- **Hero.** Clean. "Anchors on your columns hear it" is the right picture for a site survey. The before/after film needs a caption I don't have, because I can't tell what the scan desk is. For EHS it's fine: carriers, vehicles and an AMR, no people.
- **Screen 2, Where work waits.** "Scans say where it was. Tags say where it is." This lands. Block 3 lists "carrier, pallet, tote, tool and forklift", things and not people, which is good. Then this one. **Read twice:** "One team in India designs, builds and supports every part." (10 words). As the integrator, I asked myself where I sit in that. It reads as if no partner is needed.
- **Screen 3, Ask your floor.** I'm fine with the forklift idle question. It's a fleet use shown as a question, not as a result. EHS voice: I don't know the word "MCP" and don't trust it. "A person approves any action" is good.
- **Screen 4, One system.** Integrator: "shares them with your MES, ERP, WMS and AI assistants" has no protocols, no omlox hub interface and no doc link. **Read twice:** "Built on the FiRa and omlox specifications, with NXP Trimension UWB silicon inside." (13 words): a pile-up of four names. I don't trust "Runs in automotive, robotics and warehousing plants." (7 words). Which plants, and live where? I'd ask that on the first call. "Anchors need only power" is a good line, but there's no DC or PoE and no 3 to 8 m, so I still can't scope a survey from it.
- **Screen 5, Start with one bay.** "We survey the floor and plan the anchors" makes the vendor do the survey. Who commissions, and who supports after handover? It isn't said. **Read twice:** "Your MES stays in charge." (5 words): in charge of what, dispatch? I'd like six more words. "Diagnose" sounds like an audit to the EHS ear.
- **Footer.** "Safety teams: Questions." That's a FAQ. As the EHS lead, I've been sent to the back of the building. Integrators share a row with developers, and there's no partner path. "Physical AI" in the entity paragraph: I don't trust the word.

### 3. Where I'd stop
The EHS voice stops at the footer, and it's the wrong kind of stop: I scrolled the whole page waiting for the people line and got "Questions". The integrator stays to the end but leaves with nothing to resell.

### 4. Question I still have
Integrator: "Is there a partner programme, and what's in a standard install?" EHS: "Does this ever tag people, and who decides if it does?"

### 5. Score: **5/10**
It's honest and clean, with no safety overclaim and no people shown. But neither of my voices is ever spoken to.

## Concept B: "The buyer's morning" (eye-level climb past the anchor)

### 1. After the hero only
**Say-back:** "A tag goes on each trolley, bin and tool. A live map shows where it is and how long it's waited, so nobody walks the floor to find it."
**Is that right?** Yes. "Trolley, bin and tool" is the most carrier-only hero of the three, and the EHS voice notices that. The integrator voice gets the anchor on a column in the film, at 4.5 m with one power cable down the column. That is a site-survey picture.

### 2. Screen by screen
- **Nav.** "How it works · Where it helps · Your floor · Company". These are plain words, better than A's. There's still no Partners entry and no People and privacy entry in the nav itself.
- **Hero.** Best hero for me. Forklift tag "on the overhead guard" is shown as hardware, not as a result, which is right. No people.
- **Screen 2, Find it.** "The walk. The calls to stores. The second walk." Every supervisor I've worked with lives that. "Your team still decides what to do about it." Good.
- **Screen 3, Ask your floor.** "We show it live on the call" is the honest version of AMBER. I like that. I still don't know the word "MCP".
- **Screen 4, How it knows.** The integrator voice comes alive here: "Fixed to a column or wall 3 to 8 m up," "Needs only power," "Screws or sticks onto the trolley," "1 to 3 years, depending on use." I can draft a survey from this. It's missing DC or PoE, and "Sends what it knows to your MES, ERP and WMS" has no protocol and no docs link. **Read twice:** "Every trolley tells the room where it is." (8 words). It's cute, and I had to decode it. **Read twice, and it's a send-back for the EHS voice:** "This is spatial intelligence: the exact place and time of everything that moves in your plant, joined with jobs and zones." (21 words). People move in my plant. A works committee member reads that sentence aloud at the next meeting. It must say "everything you tag". **Read twice:** "One team builds every layer, so you have one contract and one support line." (14 words). Good for the plant head. For me it's the same question as A: is there room for a partner?
- **Screen 5, Start with one bay.** "Each stage has a check before the next, and you can stop at any of them." The integrator voice can sell a gated pilot. Who commissions is still only "We survey your floor". **Read twice:** "Which walk would you cut first?" (6 words). It's a nice question, but it sits as a heading over a button, and I wasn't sure what the answer was for.
- **Footer.** This is the only concept that names both my roles. "Safety: People and privacy" and "Integrators: Partners" are the two rows I came for. But the notes say the Partners row may fall back to Contact, and that would be a dead end.

### 3. Where I'd stop
The EHS voice stops hard at "everything that moves in your plant". That's the one line on any of the three pages that implies people tracking. If I keep going, the footer rescues it, but most readers won't keep going.

### 4. Question I still have
Integrator: "What does the Partners page give me: a standard kit, a responsibility matrix, versioned interface docs?" EHS: "Your footer says People and privacy. Why isn't that line on the page itself?"

### 5. Score: **6/10**
It has the best hardware story, and it's the only one that gives both my roles a door. Fix one sentence and add the boundary line, and it's an 8.

## Concept C: "One floor, seen from five heights" (split-aisle hero, scroll rise)

### 1. After the hero only
**Say-back:** "Spatial intelligence: it shows where every tagged trolley, pallet and tool is and how long it's waited."
**Is that right?** Yes. "Tagged" is in the hero, and that's the word the EHS voice wants. Then I look at the scene notes the film will follow: "Two people may walk the floor, small and unmarked." Seen from 18 m on screen 3, those are people as dots on a tracking product's map. They aren't tagged and have no trail, but nobody on the works committee will check the trail. Whether they stay is left to "if they read as CGI", which is a looks call. It has to be a policy call.

### 2. Screen by screen
- **Nav.** "Start small" is clear. "For developers (API and MCP docs)" is the only place across all three concepts that promises docs by name, so the integrator voice notices it. There's no Partners entry, and no safety or privacy entry.
- **Hero.** The split down the aisle line is clever and works on a phone. For EHS it's fine as long as no people are in this frame.
- **Screen 2, down to the trolley.** Best hardware line on any page: "No data or sync cables to run. Anchors need only power, DC or PoE." I'd paste that into a quote. "Down to ±10 cm, with AmIMo anchors. Your site sets the final figure." That's honest. There's no mounting height here, but the column at 5 m is in the picture.
- **Screen 3, the rise.** Strong for the plant head. For EHS this is where the walking people turn into specks on a map. Send it back unless they're cut.
- **Screen 4, Ask your floor.** **Read twice:** "AmIHub keeps the place and time of everything tagged, joined with your zones, so people, your systems and AI assistants read the same floor." (24 words). On the first pass my eye joined "everything tagged" and "people", and only on the second did I see that people are the readers. Move "people" away from "tagged". "Physical AI": I don't trust the word.
- **Screen 5, Start with one bay.** Integrator: "Talks to MES, ERP and WMS over REST, MQTT and WebSocket." Finally there are protocols on the page, but still no omlox hub interface wording and no version. **Read twice:** "When it earns its place, it grows across the plant on the same AmIHub." (14 words): who decides it has earned it? And "Runs in automotive manufacturing, robotics and warehousing." is the same distrust as in A.
- **Footer.** This one has no safety row, no partner row and no integrator row. Of the three concepts, this footer is the worst for my roles.

### 3. Where I'd stop
The EHS voice stops at screen 3 if the walking people are visible in the rise. Otherwise I'd stop at the footer, which has nothing for my roles.

### 4. Question I still have
EHS: "Why are there people in your floor picture, and what does your system see of them?" Integrator: "Where's the partner path? Your developer docs are named, but who commissions?"

### 5. Score: **4/10**
It has the best interface and power lines of the three. It also has the only scene I'd send back for EHS, and no door for either of my roles.

## 6. Ranking: B, A, C
1. **B** is the only one that names safety and integrators in the footer, and it has the most survey-ready hardware screen. One sentence ("everything that moves") breaks it, and that's fixable.
2. **A** never crosses my lines. It also never speaks to me: safety goes to "Questions" and integrators get lumped in with developers.
3. **C** has the strongest integrator facts (DC or PoE, REST/MQTT/WebSocket, docs in the nav). It puts people on a tracking map and gives neither role a door.

## 7. Best and worst lines
**Best:** "No data or sync cables to run. Anchors need only power, DC or PoE." (Concept C, screen 2.) An integrator can quote it and a site survey can use it, and it overclaims nothing.
**Worst:** "This is spatial intelligence: the exact place and time of everything that moves in your plant, joined with jobs and zones." (Concept B, screen 4.) People move in my plant. The line implies people tracking, and it's on the page I otherwise rank first.

## 8. One change that would make B a 10
On screen 5, under the pilot paragraph, add one plain two-card strip called "Who does what, and where the line is" (Apple Privacy style, no icons):
- **The line:** "The pilot tags carriers, not people. Tracking people is a separate scope, with its own policy and consent." Link it to People and privacy.
- **Who does what:** "Standard kit: tag, anchor, AmIHub. We survey and commission. You or your integrator support it, with versioned interface docs, including the omlox hub interface." Link it to Partners. That page must exist at launch and must not fall back to Contact.

Then change the screen 4 line to "everything you tag". With that, the integrator forwards it to his sales head, and the EHS lead forwards it to the plant head with no objection.

Leave the bracketed parts as open questions for the Truth and Quality Lead and the founders. Nothing on this site has yet confirmed who commissions, who supports, or the partner model.
