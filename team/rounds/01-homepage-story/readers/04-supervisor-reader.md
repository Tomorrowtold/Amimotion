# Reader panel, round 1: P4 Supervisor Reader

SIMULATION. This is a hypothesis buyer from kit 09 section 6. No real supervisor was interviewed. I am reading as a line-side logistics supervisor at a Tier 1 plant. It is the end of a twelve-hour shift and I am on a mid-range phone with a cracked screen. I read sections 3 and 4 only.

My questions going in: Will it save me the walk? Does it track me or the trolley? Who sees the alert first? Does it work on my phone? What if the tag falls off? Do I have to scan anything?

---

## Concept A

### 1. After the hero only
"It is a small tag on the trolley and boxes on the columns, and a map shows where the trolley is and how long it is standing."
Mostly right. But the first thing I read is "spatial intelligence", and I do not know that word. I got the meaning from the third sentence, not the first.

### 2. Screen by screen
- **Nav.** "Platform, Uses, Pilot, Learn, Company." These are office words. "Diagnose your floor" sounds like a doctor. On my phone it folds to a menu anyway, so I do not care much.
- **Hero.** "Where work is. Where it waits." Short. Good. "AmIMotion is spatial intelligence for factories and warehouses." I did not know the word. **Read twice:** "One live map shows where each thing is now and how long it has stood still." (16 words.) "Stood still" is a word I use, so I trust that part. Film: no people, only trolleys and a tugger. Good. The 3 cm tag on a trolley rail in close-up is the first time I believe it. The before shot with the scan desks is not my problem. My problem is the walk, and nobody walks in this film.
- **Screen 2, "Scans say where it was. Tags say where it is."** Clever, but the line plays on words. I had to read it twice to catch the trick. "So someone walks the floor to find it." Yes. That someone is me.
  - Block 1, "Find it from a screen.": "A trolley left the press an hour ago. Nobody can say where it went." This is my day. "The tag reports itself, so nobody feeds the system by hand." Good. That answers "do I have to scan". This is the block I would show my line leader. But the picture is a render of the floor, not my phone. I want to see the search on a phone.
  - Block 2, "Every wait, timed.": "You hear at the morning review that a job ran late." That is my boss's meeting, not mine. "Finding out why stays your team's call." Good. It does not blame my operators.
  - Block 3, "The whole plant on one map.": manager talk. **Read twice:** "Now every tagged carrier, pallet, tote, tool and forklift shows on one map." (13 words, a list.) "One team in India designs, builds and supports every part." Fine, but not for me.
- **Screen 3, "Ask your floor a question."** The forklift question is a real question, but it is the forklift in-charge's question, not mine. Mine is "trolley kahan hai?" Then "MCP server". **Word I did not know:** MCP. "Each assistant gets its own access. Every call is logged." Logged who? Logging me? I lost trust here. "A person approves any action." Good.
- **Screen 4, "One team builds every layer."** **Read twice:** "AmIHub turns what the anchors hear into positions, zones and waits. Then it shares them with your MES, ERP, WMS and AI assistants." **Read twice:** "Built on the FiRa and omlox specifications, with NXP Trimension UWB silicon inside." (13 words. FiRa, omlox, NXP, Trimension and UWB are five words I do not know.) "Down to ±10 cm" means nothing to me unless the trolley is found. No battery life anywhere. The tag is "stuck flat" with glue. My trolleys get washed and bumped. Will glue hold?
- **Screen 5, "Start with one bay."** "You bring the flow you want to see and one contact on the floor." The contact on the floor is me. Good that I am named as part of it. "Your MES stays in charge." I do not care. "Not slides" is good.
- **Footer.** "Shift supervisors: Find the asset." I see my job named. Good. "Asset" is a finance word. On the floor we say trolley.

### 3. Where I would stop
At screen 3, on "AmIHub has an MCP server built in." Three lines that are not for me, plus the word "logged". If I keep going, I stop for good at the FiRa line on screen 4.

### 4. The question I still have
Does it work on my phone, and who gets told first when a trolley waits too long, me or my manager?

### 5. Score: 5/10
Block 1 on screen 2 is for me. The rest is written for my plant head and IT.

## Concept B

### 1. After the hero only
"It puts a small tag on each trolley, bin and tool, and I find it on a screen instead of walking."
That is right. "You find out from a screen, not a walk." That sentence is my whole life.

### 2. Screen by screen
- **Nav.** "How it works, Where it helps, Your floor, Company." I can read these. "Your floor" feels like it is talking to me.
- **Hero.** "A small tag rides on each trolley, bin and tool." Trolley first. Good. "You find out from a screen, not a walk." **This is the line I would read aloud to my crew.** Film: it starts at my eye height and I cannot see my own floor. That is true; I live behind racks. The camera goes up past the box on the column. Now I know where the anchor lives without being told. The tag is screwed on through its ears, so it will not fall off as easily. No people. Good. "Spatial intelligence" again in sentence one, but the rest saves it.
- **Screen 2, "Find it from a screen, not a walk."** "Think of the last time someone went looking for a trolley. The walk. The calls to stores. The second walk." Yes. Short and true. "The second walk" hurts because it is true. **Read twice:** "The map shows where it is, when it was last seen and how long it has stood there." (17 words, but every word is plain.) "Last seen" is right, and I trust it more than "is now". "Your team still decides what to do about it." Good. The picture is a real screen, not a drawing. But it has a cursor and typing on a computer. I do not sit at a computer. Show it on a phone.
- **Screen 3, "Ask your floor a question."** "Which trolleys have waited at inspection for more than an hour?" That is a trolley question. Closer to mine. **Word I did not know:** MCP. **Read twice:** "AmIHub has a built-in MCP server, so the assistant your team already uses can connect." (15 words.) The assistant my team already uses is WhatsApp. "Every request is logged." Again, logged whose request? "We show it live on the call." Good, honest.
- **Screen 4, "Every trolley tells the room where it is."** **Read twice:** the headline. A trolley does not tell a room anything. I got it the second time. The three blocks are the best part of all three concepts for day-one questions: it screws or sticks on, the battery lasts "1 to 3 years, depending on use", it sleeps when it stops, the anchor sits 3 to 8 m up and needs only power. That answers "will the tag fall off" and "who changes batteries". Then the last paragraph. **Read twice:** "This is spatial intelligence: the exact place and time of everything that moves in your plant, joined with jobs and zones." (21 words.) "Everything that moves in your plant." I move in my plant. My operators move. **This line reads as people tracking.** "First layer of Physical AI" is consultant English. **Word I did not trust:** Physical AI.
- **Screen 5, "Start with one bay. Grow to the plant."** "Each stage has a check before the next, and you can stop at any of them." This is for my boss, but it calms me too. Systems leave here without a goodbye; this one says you can stop. "Your bay builds itself as you answer." I would try it on my phone. "Which walk would you cut first?" Good. That is my question back at me.
- **Footer.** "Supervisor: Find it from a screen." My job, in my words. "Safety: People and privacy." That page should be linked higher up, for me, not only for safety.

### 3. Where I would stop
At the last paragraph of screen 4, on "everything that moves in your plant". I stop and I start to worry. Before that I was nodding.

### 4. The question I still have
Who sees the alert first when a trolley waits too long? And does the trolley search open on my phone, or only on the office PC?

### 5. Score: 6/10
It sees my walk. It does not show me my phone, and one line hints that it watches people.

## Concept C

### 1. After the hero only
"It shows where each tagged trolley, pallet and tool is and how long it has waited."
That is right, and it is the plainest of the three. But it never says "you walk less", so I know what it is, not why I want it.

### 2. Screen by screen
- **Nav.** "How it works, Where it helps, Start small, Company." Fine. "Start small" is honest.
- **Hero.** "Where work is. Where it waits." on two lines. Good on a phone. **Read twice:** "AmIMotion shows where every tagged trolley, pallet and tool is, and how long it has waited." (16 words.) Plain words, but long. The picture is split by the yellow aisle line: plain floor on the left, blue and amber on the right. On a cracked phone screen at 1:1 I am not sure I would see the split. I might just see a busy floor. The idea is smart. On my screen it may not work.
- **Screen 2, "Your trolleys say where they are."** Good headline. Plain. "An AmIGo tag rides on each trolley, pallet or tote. AmIMo anchors on the columns hear it. AmIHub puts it on the map." Three short sentences. I can say that back. "Tags sleep when still, so a battery lasts 1 to 3 years, depending on use." Good. Screwed onto a steel plate. Good. **Word I did not know:** PoE. "DC or PoE" is for the electrician.
- **Screen 3, "See where work waits."** "Every zone entry gets a time." OK. "A long wait shows you where to look. Why it waited stays your team's call." **This is the second best line I read today.** It does not blame my operator. The camera rising as I scroll is nice, but on an old phone it may stutter. I see the whole plant from 18 m. That is my manager's view. I do not need the whole plant. I need C-014.
- **Screen 4, "Ask your floor a question."** "Which trolleys have waited at inspection for more than an hour?" Good, trolley question. **Read twice:** "AmIHub keeps the place and time of everything tagged, joined with your zones, so people, your systems and AI assistants read the same floor." (25 words, the longest sentence I read today.) "First layer of Physical AI." Consultant. **Word I did not know:** MCP. "Every call is logged." Logged again.
- **Screen 5, "Start with one bay."** "When it earns its place, it grows across the plant." Good. Then **read twice:** "Talks to MES, ERP and WMS over REST, MQTT and WebSocket." (10 words, and six of them I do not know.) This is the IT page, not the homepage. "One team, one contract, one support line." Fine.
- **Footer.** Four columns of links. No row for my job. "Tools and fixtures (find it from a screen)" is in the nav, under a heading that is not about trolleys.
- **The scene.** The brief says "Two people may walk the floor, small and unmarked." I know they are not tagged because they have no trail. My crew will not know that. They will see two people next to blue tracked things and ask "are we next?" In concepts A and B there are no people, so nobody asks. This one is a risk, not a fail, unless they cut the people.

### 3. Where I would stop
At screen 4, on the 25-word "This is spatial intelligence..." paragraph. Screen 3 is the high point and the page goes downhill after it.

### 4. The question I still have
Can I find one trolley on my phone? This page shows me the plant from the sky, but never one search for one trolley.

### 5. Score: 4/10
Clean and honest, and it has the best truth line. But it is a manager's page. There is no walk, no search for a trolley, and two people in the scene.

---

## 6. Ranking: B, A, C

1. **B** starts from my walk, my trolley and my eye height, and its screen 4 answers my day-one questions: screws on, 1 to 3 years, power only.
2. **A** has one block written for me ("A trolley left the press an hour ago"), but the rest talks over my head (MCP, FiRa, omlox, NXP) and it never gives the battery life.
3. **C** is the plainest to read, but it looks at my plant from 18 m up, never searches for one trolley, and puts people in the scene.

## 7. Best and worst lines

- **Best:** "You find out from a screen, not a walk." (B, hero)
- **Worst:** "Talks to MES, ERP and WMS over REST, MQTT and WebSocket." (C, screen 5). A close second, and the more dangerous one for me: "the exact place and time of everything that moves in your plant" (B, screen 4), because it reads as people tracking.

## 8. One change to make B a 10

Make screen 2 a phone in a hand: the real AmIHub search, I type a trolley name, it answers "Last seen 10:42, inspection, waiting 1 h 10 min" in under 20 seconds. Then put one plain line under it: "We tag the trolley, not you." Also change "everything that moves" on screen 4 to "every tagged trolley, bin and tool". That is the same fix, so the page never hints that it watches people.

Still open for every concept: none of them says who gets the alert first. If it reaches my manager before me, I am against it from day one.
