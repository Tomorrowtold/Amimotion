# Reader panel, round 1: Production Control Reader (P2)

SIMULATION. This is a modelled buyer from the profile, not an interview.

How I read it: on my phone, standing outside the PPC cabin at about 10:15. The 7 o'clock plan is already wrong, a trolley of CNC housings is missing between washing and inspection, and I have ten minutes before the next call from dispatch. I read only the screens and the nav.

What I came for, in my words: "How fast can I find a trolley?" "Will it tell me before the job is late?" "Who gets the alert?" "Do I have to scan anything?" "How does it know which job is on the trolley?" "Does it feed my Excel or MES, or is it one more screen?"

## Concept A

### 1. After the hero only
Say-back: "A small tag goes on things that move, boxes on the columns pick it up, and a map shows where each thing is and how long it has been standing."

Is that right? Mostly. It says where and it says standing time, which is half my job. It does not say trolley, it does not say job, and it does not say who gets told. "Where each thing is now" is a promise I do not trust: if the tag sleeps when the trolley is parked, then "now" is really "last seen at". The film's "before" with the scan desks is the most honest picture of my day in all three concepts. The 10:17 scan and the 10:18 trolley that disappeared behind the rack, that is exactly it.

### 2. Screen by screen
- **Nav.** Six words and a link. "Uses" is not a word I would say. I would not open "Platform". Fine to skip.
- **Hero.** Felt: "OK, a map." The H1 is good. Read twice: "AmIMotion is spatial intelligence for factories and warehouses." (8 words). "Spatial intelligence" means nothing to me; I read it again to see if I missed a noun I know. Did not trust: "now" in "where each thing is now".
- **Screen 2, Where work waits.** Felt: this is the one for me. "Scans say where it was. Tags say where it is." is sharp and does not insult my barcode system. Block 1 is my morning: "A trolley left the press an hour ago. Nobody can say where it went." And "when it last moved" plus "nobody feeds the system by hand" answers my scan question without me asking. Block 2, "A long wait shows while there is still time to act", is the line I want, but shows to whom? On which screen? Who acts? Nobody is named. Block 3 is the dashboard block: "You see how work really moves across bays and docks" is for my plant head, and "One team in India designs, builds and supports every part" is stuck on the end of a block about flow and made me stop. Read twice: "One team in India designs, builds and supports every part." (10 words) because I thought "part" meant my part. Did not trust: "where it is" in the headline, same stale-data issue as the hero.
- **Screen 3, Ask your floor.** Felt: drift. The question is about forklifts. I do not run forklifts, I run jobs. Then MCP. Did not know: "MCP", "speaks MCP". Read twice: "AmIHub has an MCP server built in. Any assistant that speaks MCP can use it." (15 words). This screen is for IT, not for me.
- **Screen 4, One system.** Felt: a spec sheet. ±10 cm, FiRa, omlox, NXP Trimension. I do not need 10 cm, I need "which bay". "Then it shares them with your MES, ERP, WMS" is the only line here I care about, and it is buried in sentence four. Did not know: "FiRa", "omlox", "Trimension".
- **Screen 5, Start with one bay.** Felt: relief. "You bring the flow you want to see and one contact on the floor. Your MES stays in charge." Good, bounded, I could sell that. "Six questions… before anyone calls you" is fair.
- **Footer.** "Production and planning: Work in progress." Fine. But "How tags link to jobs" is sent to MES owners, not to me. That is the one page I most need.

### 3. Where I would have stopped
At screen 3. The forklift question and MCP told me the page had moved on from my job. I would have scrolled straight to "Start with one bay" if I scrolled at all.

### 4. The question I still have
"When the trolley sits at washing too long, who gets told, and how? And how does it know that trolley has job 4471 on it?"

### 5. Score
**6 / 10.** Screen 2 block 1 is close to what I want. No owner for the wait, no association story, "now" for old readings, and three screens for other people.

## Concept B

### 1. After the hero only
Say-back: "A tag on each trolley, bin and tool puts it on a live map with how long it has waited, so you find it from a screen instead of walking."

Is that right? Yes, and it is the only hero that names a trolley in the first breath and names my pain: "You find out from a screen, not a walk." I could say that to my plant head. Still missing: who gets told, and the job on the trolley.

### 2. Screen by screen
- **Nav.** "How it works · Where it helps · Your floor · Company". I understand every word. "Your floor" pulls me.
- **Hero.** Felt: "This is my morning." Eye-level shot where a machine hides half the trolleys, that is what I see from the PPC cabin door. Read twice: "AmIMotion is spatial intelligence for factories and warehouses." (8 words), same reason as A. Did not trust: nothing big. "How long it has waited" is right.
- **Screen 2, Find it.** Felt: yes. "The walk. The calls to stores. The second walk." I have done the second walk this week. A real screen capture of the search, typed at human speed, landing on one amber trolley with "last seen" and "time in zone": that is the AirTag moment, and I would want to try it before lunch. "Your team still decides what to do about it" is honest. Did not trust: searching "Gearbox housings" quietly assumes the system knows what is on the trolley. Nobody told me how it knows. If I tag a trolley, I know where the trolley is, not the housings. That needs one line.
- **Screen 3, Ask your floor.** Felt: this is my question, word for word: "Which trolleys have waited at inspection for more than an hour?" Good. But I have to ask it. I want it to tell me before I think to ask. And "A person approves any action" — which person? Did not know: "MCP server". Read twice: "AmIHub has a built-in MCP server, so the assistant your team already uses can connect." (16 words). My team does not use an assistant. We use WhatsApp.
- **Screen 4, How it knows.** Felt: I could follow this without knowing what UWB is. Tag sends a pulse when it moves and sleeps when it stops. Anchor on a column hears it. AmIHub times every wait and "Sends what it knows to your MES, ERP and WMS. Your MES stays in charge." That answers "is it another screen" well enough. Read twice: "Every trolley tells the room where it is." (8 words). "Tells the room"? The room does not listen, the anchor does. Read twice: "This is spatial intelligence: the exact place and time of everything that moves in your plant, joined with jobs and zones." (21 words). "Everything that moves" is too big; it is everything tagged. "Joined with jobs" is the first mention of jobs on the whole page, and it says nothing about how. Did not know: "Physical AI", "FiRa", "omlox".
- **Screen 5, Start with one bay.** Felt: I would forward this screen. "Each stage has a check before the next, and you can stop at any of them." My plant head will like that. "Which walk would you cut first?" is a good question to put to me; I have an answer. The diagnostic where my bay fills in sounds like play first. Good.
- **Footer.** "Production control: Work in progress." My exact title. Good.

### 3. Where I would have stopped
I would not have stopped. Screen 4's paragraph slowed me, but by then I wanted to see screen 5.

### 4. The question I still have
"When two trolleys sit at inspection past the limit, does someone get a message on their phone, and is it the inspection supervisor or me? And who links the job card to the trolley, and does that mean one more scan for my people?"

### 5. Score
**7 / 10.** I would forward it to my plant head today with one line, and he would reply "ask them about alerts". It finds my trolley. It does not yet tell me before it is late, or who acts.

My one-line WhatsApp: "Sir, see this, they find the trolley on screen instead of walking the floor, pilot on one bay, MES untouched. Worth a call?" Fits one line.

## Concept C

### 1. After the hero only
Say-back: "Spatial intelligence that shows where every tagged trolley, pallet and tool is and how long it has waited."

Is that right? Right words, and "tagged" is the most honest word in any of the three heroes. But it reads like a definition, not my day. No walk, no search, no "you". The split-floor picture is clever but I am not sure on my phone that I would see the trick in one look.

### 2. Screen by screen
- **Nav.** "How it works · Where it helps · Start small · Company". Clear. "Start small" is good.
- **Hero.** Felt: polite, a bit cold. Read twice: "Spatial intelligence for factories and warehouses." (6 words, no verb, so I looked for one).
- **Screen 2, Your trolleys say where they are.** Felt: why are you showing me the hardware already? ±10 cm, battery 1 to 3 years, DC or PoE. This is the OT engineer's screen, second on the page. "Your trolleys say where they are" is the same stale-data problem as A: say "where they were last seen". Did not know: "PoE". Did not trust: nothing false, just not for me yet.
- **Screen 3, See where work waits.** Felt: this is my screen. "You see how long a trolley sat at inspection, or a pallet in a staging lane." "Why it waited stays your team's call" is honest. But the picture is a camera rising to a map with amber clusters. That is a heatmap. Pretty, and no action. Who goes to the cluster?
- **Screen 4, Ask your floor a question.** Felt: good question, same as B. Then the paragraph. Read twice: "AmIHub keeps the place and time of everything tagged, joined with your zones, so people, your systems and AI assistants read the same floor." (25 words). Read twice: "It is the first layer of Physical AI for factories and warehouses." (12 words). Did not know: "Physical AI", "MCP".
- **Screen 5, Start with one bay.** Felt: bounded, fine, but then a list of REST, MQTT, WebSocket. Did not know: "REST", "MQTT", "WebSocket". "Runs on a PC inside your plant" I like. "When it earns its place" is a good phrase.
- **Footer.** Just columns. Nowhere says "Production control: go here". I would have to guess "Work in progress".

### 3. Where I would have stopped
At screen 2. The page went to specs before it showed me finding anything. If I came back, I would only come back for screen 3.

### 4. The question I still have
"Can I search for one trolley and find it? I never saw that happen. And who gets the alert?"

### 5. Score
**5 / 10.** Honest wording, nice pictures, but no one finds a trolley on this page. It shows me a map and asks me to admire it.

## 6. Ranking for me
**B, then A, then C.**
B is the only one that shows my task being done: someone types, the map lands on the waiting trolley, "last seen" and "time in zone", and it asks my question at inspection.
A has the truest picture of my pain (the scan desks and the gap between them) and the "nobody feeds the system by hand" line, but it drifts to forklifts, MCP and specs for three screens.
C is the most careful with words and the least about my job: hardware second, a heatmap third, and nobody ever finds a trolley.

## 7. Best and worst lines
- **Best:** "Think of the last time someone went looking for a trolley. The walk. The calls to stores. The second walk." (B, screen 2). Close second: "You find out from a screen, not a walk." (B, hero).
- **Worst:** "AmIHub has a built-in MCP server, so the assistant your team already uses can connect." (B, screen 3). My team uses WhatsApp and a printed plan. This line tells me the page was not written for me. Runner-up: "Every trolley tells the room where it is." (B, screen 4).

## 8. One change that makes B a 10 for me
Add the missing half of the job to screen 2, in the same real-screen style, right after the search: **the wait that tells someone.** Show one trolley pass its limit at inspection, and the alert land with a named role ("Inspection supervisor, line 2") on a phone, with the line "Last seen at Inspection at 10:42. Waiting 52 min, limit 30." And add one sentence that tells the association story straight: "Link the job card to the trolley once when it leaves the CNC cell. After that, the job's status follows the trolley." Then I can say in one breath: "It finds my trolleys and tells the right person what is waiting before it is late." That is the line I would forward.

### Cross-concept flags for the makers (production-control reasons)
- No concept shows an exception going to a named owner. All three stop at "a long wait shows". That is visibility with no owner.
- No concept tells me how location becomes job status (tag to carrier to job). B's "joined with jobs" and A's footer link are the only traces, and neither shows the step.
- Stale-data wording fails in A ("where each thing is now", "Tags say where it is") and C ("Your trolleys say where they are"). B passes with "when it was last seen".
- B's "everything that moves in your plant" (screen 4) breaks the tagged-carriers rule; it should read "everything you tag".
- None says barcodes or RFID fail. Good.
