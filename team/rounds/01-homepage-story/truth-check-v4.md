What "complete" means for this check. Where a founders' document states a fact, it may ship at its level in kit 03. Where the documents are silent, or two of them disagree, "complete" does not fill the gap. The page ships the safe true line, and the conflict is logged (charter: nobody picks quietly).

Rulings: **PASS** ships as written. **REWORD** ships only in the words given. **STOP** does not ship. The reason and source are given. Where a stopped clause has a safe line that passes, that line is given, and it may ship in its place. A stop on a claim cannot be overruled (charter).

The truth rulings printed inside homepage-v4.md's claim tables are the committee's draft. Where they differ from this file, this file holds.

### 2.3 "The consent of the people tagged" (V81 to V83)

The founders' documents do not separate people from the product. [B3] sells a personnel badge. [B5] day one shows "every tagged asset, vehicle and person". [B6] gives the EHS lead restricted-zone alerts. [B2] calls DL-TDoA "privacy-preserving". None states a consent policy. The only source for the v4 wording is kit 09, a team note, which says "its own policy and consent conversation (HYPOTHESIS on how plants will handle it)" and lists "how consent was handled (ask)" as unknown. "The consent of the people tagged" is stronger than that: it commits AmIMotion to individual consent, and a reader will take it as a legal statement no founder made. STOP on that clause. What passes: "The pilot tags trolleys, bins and pallets, not people. Tagging people would be a separate project. It would need its own policy." Logged as X17.

### 2.4 "On a screen or on your phone" (V12, V13)

v4 argues that C18 names Fleet Manager and Spatial AI, not mobile views. That reads C18 too narrowly. C18 version 1 is the whole [B6] page, "AmISense and AmIFleet presented as current". The mobile views are on that page, in its "Delivered as" line. [B2] puts "Web · mobile · API" under the Act column (Analytics), not under AmIHub Core. [B5], the Core page, never mentions a phone. So the line puts an Analytics feature of open maturity onto "AmIHub's live map". "Look it up on AmIHub's live map instead." passes. The clause is stopped until a founder says mobile views are live (X14).

### 2.5 Zone limits sending events (V24, V25, V100, V107)

[B5] states three things: you can "set rules", every dwell event is "time-stamped and queryable", and "zone and fence events" stream "to any system you name". It does not state the step between them: that a dwell limit, once passed, makes an event and sends it out. That step is the dwell exception. Q6 asks whether AmIHub can create one. [CW] marks the "exception interface" NEEDS VALIDATION (K03 RED). [FB], dated 21 Oct and later than every kit file, still asks the founders for "exact wording for dwell alerts". [B7]'s "waited over 15 minutes" is an agent "act" example (K03 AMBER: an example, not a feature). What passes, in [B5]'s own words: "Set rules for each zone. AmIHub streams zone events to any system you name." X6 narrows to this and no further (X15).

### 2.6 The second-plant line (V86)

[B5] gives "multi-site from one console" in its indicative table, and K03 treats that table as indicative. [CW] says "multi-site operation" is a separate scope and "a second site may need a different mounting/network design". The page also says AmIHub runs on a PC inside the plant with no internet. How one console reaches a second plant is not stated anywhere, and the cloud model is open (C21). "Joins" says it as a step already done. "Can run" says only what the brochure gives. "Its own survey" holds on [B4] and [CW]. Allowed: "A second plant gets its own survey, and AmIHub can run both from one console." Logged as X16.
