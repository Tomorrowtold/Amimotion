## The shared world (every floor picture)

One model hall, built once in Blender, rendered at 8K. Camera A locked: three-quarter overhead, about 45 degrees down, about 11 m up, near 35 mm.

The hall is a designed model, never a photograph. One flat matte material per kind of thing. Simplified shapes. No wear, grime, tyre marks, patched slabs or faded paint. No daylight realism, sun patches or depth of field on wide shots. No people. No signs, text, brands or numbers. Blue trails and amber tints are flat graphic marks. Nothing suggests a real or named site.

Colours: floor #f6f4ef, machines #e4e2dc, shadow #c9c7c1, hardware #3a3d42, located blue #66a5fa, waiting amber #f0a43a. Fixed counts: three trolleys wait in the inspection queue and one pallet waits in the staging lane.

No AmIHub screen is drawn anywhere. A real AmIHub capture replaces a fallback only on the day one may be shown. No mock-up of the UI, ever.

## Screen 5. One system

> ## Three parts work as one system.
>
> **AmIGo tag.** Screws or sticks onto the trolley. Sends a short UWB radio pulse when it moves and sleeps when it stops. While it sleeps, the map shows where it was last seen. If it comes off the trolley, the map still shows its last-seen spot. One battery lasts 1 to 3 years, depending on use. AmIHub tracks each tag's health and battery level.
>
> **AmIMo anchor.** Fixed to a column or wall 3 to 8 m up. Records when each pulse arrives and the angle it came from. Needs only power, from DC or PoE. It syncs over the air, with no sync cable. It sends its data to AmIHub over Wi-Fi, so it needs no separate data cable. Ethernet is also an option. Each reaches about 50 m around it in clear line of sight. Racking and steel change that, so we survey your floor and set the count. With these anchors, AmIHub places a tag down to ±10 cm. How close depends on your floor. You set anchors up from a phone app. AmIHub finds each one on its own and raises an alert if one goes dark.
>
> **AmIHub.** Works out where each tag is from those times and angles. Runs on an industrial PC inside your plant, with no internet needed. Your MES, ERP and WMS connect to it through open REST, MQTT, WebSocket and omlox hub interfaces. Your team, your systems and AI assistants read the same live map. Your MES stays in charge of every job. AmIHub adds where its trolley is and how long it has waited.
>
> Built on the FiRa and omlox specifications.
>
> [How it locates a tag](/technology/) · [Specifications](/specs/) · [For developers](/developers/)

## Screen 6. Start with one bay

> ## Start with one bay.
>
> A pilot follows one flow in one bay or line. You choose the flow. The pilot times today's waits first, over a window you choose. That is your baseline. Before it starts, we agree how to count a wait that runs past shift end. We also agree who on your team acts when work waits too long.
>
> The pilot tags trolleys, bins and pallets, not people. Tagging people would be a separate project. It would need its own policy. You also set how long the map keeps its history.
>
> You see your own waits in one bay, and prove the numbers, before you decide on more. Then grow plant-wide on the same AmIHub, without re-fitting the first bay. A second plant gets its own survey, and AmIHub can run both from one console.
>

One contract and one support line cover every layer. Your team or your integrator can build on it at any layer, through open interfaces.
