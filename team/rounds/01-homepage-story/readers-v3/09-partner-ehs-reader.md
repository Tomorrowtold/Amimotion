## 1. After the hero only

**Say-back:** "A tag goes on each trolley, bin and tool, anchors on the columns hear it, and one live map shows where it went and how long it has waited, so nobody walks the floor to find it."

The hero still doesn't say it doesn't track people. But screen 2 now says it nine seconds later, so I don't have to wait until screen 6.

## 7. Best and worst lines

**Best:** "It goes on the trolley, not on the person pushing it." (Screen 2.) Eleven words. A works committee member can read it aloud, and it's on screen 2, not screen 6.

**Worst:** "Tagging people is a separate decision, and yours." (Screen 6.) It nearly draws the line, and then hands the pen to management. For an EHS lead, "yours" without "consent" is the one word on this page a works committee could use against it.

## 8. One change that would make it a 10

On screen 6, make two sentences do the full job:
- **People:** "Tagging people is a separate decision, with its own policy and the consent of the people tagged." This replaces "and yours".
- **Integrators:** after "One team owns every layer...", write: "We commission the pilot. Your integrator can extend it on our versioned interface docs." Change the footer row to "Integrators" pointing at a real partner page, never at Contact.

The wording for both is an open question for the Truth and Quality Lead and the founders. Nothing I read confirms a consent policy, who commissions, or whether integrators may support the system. Name no partner and show no logo until that is settled.
