# Reader panel, round 1 v3: P4 Supervisor Reader

SIMULATION. This is a hypothesis buyer from kit 09 section 6. No real supervisor was interviewed. I am reading as a line-side logistics supervisor at a Tier 1 plant. It is the end of a twelve-hour shift and I am on a mid-range phone with a cracked screen. I read sections 3 and 4 of homepage v3 only.

My questions going in: Will it save me the walk? Does it track me or the trolley? Who sees the alert first? Does it work on my phone? What if the tag falls off? Do I have to scan anything?

---

## 1. After the hero only

"A small tag goes on each trolley, the boxes on the columns hear it, and one map shows me where it went and how long it has been waiting, so I don't walk."

That is right. "You find out from a screen, not a walk." is still there. "Where it went and how long it has waited" is better than v2's "where it is", because it is what I actually ask on the floor: kahan hai, and kab se pada hai. "Spatial intelligence" is still the first thing I hit. I still don't know the word. I still skip it. Five short sentences, longest 12 words. I read the hero once.

## 2. Screen by screen

- **Nav.** Same as v2. Four plain words and a menu. I would not open it. Fine.
- **Hero.** Short, trolley first, no people in the film. Trolleys on amber on my phone crop: those are waiting, I get it in one look. The "before" still shows checkpoint desks, not a man walking. The copy carries the walk, so I let it go again.
- **Screen 2, "A scan tells you where it passed. A tag tells you where it stopped."** Better than v2. "Stopped" is honest: a trolley behind the racks is stopped, and that is where I lose it. "The walk. The calls to stores. The second walk." Still my day. Then the line I asked for, on the screen I actually read: "It goes on the trolley, not on the person pushing it." That is the line. Floor words, no "carrier", and it is on screen 2, not screen 6. "You see where it was last seen, and when." I trust that. "Nobody scans it along the way" answers my scan question. **Read twice:** "The tag sends a pulse as it moves, so nobody scans it along the way." (15 words.) "Pulse" made me think of a heartbeat for a second. **Still not trusted:** the phone. The capture brief still says "if AmIHub runs in a phone browser... if not, record the desktop". So the page still does not tell me if it opens on my phone. Better that it does not lie, but I still do not know.
- **Screen 3, "See where work waits."** "Why it waited stays your team's call." Still the truest line here. No blame on my operators. "Your supervisor can see it during the shift, not at the next morning's review." Two feelings. Good: it is me who sees it, during the shift, before the morning meeting where I get shouted at. Bad: "your supervisor" means this page is talking to my boss about me. I am the one being handed a screen, so I will take it. **Read twice:** "Link each trolley to its job, and its wait becomes the job's wait." (13 words.) I had to work out "the job's wait". **Office words:** "We agree with you how to link them before the pilot." Who is "you"? Not me. **Word I half know:** "zone". We say "area" or the station name.
- **Screen 4, "Ask your floor a question."** "Which trolleys have waited at inspection for more than an hour?" A real trolley question. "Asking changes nothing." Three words, very clear, I like it. "Any change, such as raising a work order, needs a person to approve it." OK, but which person? The MCP block is set small and told for IT. I skip it. Right.
- **Screen 5, "Three parts work as one system."** I read the AmIGo block only. "Screws or sticks onto the trolley." "Sends a short pulse when it moves and sleeps when it stops." "While it sleeps, the map shows where it was last seen." "One battery lasts 1 to 3 years, depending on use." Four of my questions in four sentences. I glanced at the anchor block because it is huge (86 words): "How close depends on your floor." Fixed from v2's "final figure", I get it now. "If an anchor goes dark, AmIHub flags it." Good, but flags it to who? **Words I did not know:** PoE, ±10 cm, REST, MQTT, WebSocket, omlox, FiRa, MES, WMS. Not my block. **Still missing:** what if the tag falls off the trolley? Screwed or stuck, a forklift bump happens every week.
- **Screen 6, "Start with one bay."** "A pilot follows one flow." "Follows", not "watches". Fixed. "The pilot tags trolleys, bins and pallets, not people." Floor words now. Fixed. **Then this, and it made me stop:** "Tagging people is a separate decision, and yours." "Yours" is my plant head, not me. My crew will read it as: they can tag us later if the boss decides. Screen 2 closed that door. This line opens it again. **Read twice:** "Before it starts, we agree with you how each wait counts, even one still open at shift end." (18 words, the longest I read.) **Read twice:** "The first waits it times are today's, so you can use them as your baseline." (15 words.) "Baseline" is a manager word. The "What you buy" block, NDA, integrator, more than one site: not for me, I skip. "You can stop at any of them" is still gone. I still miss it.
- **Footer.** "Shift supervisors: Where is that trolley?" Still my link, in my words. "Physical AI", "NXP Trimension UWB silicon": consultant English, but at the very bottom. I do not read it.

## 3. Where I would stop

Same as v2. I read screens 1 to 4 to the end. On screen 5 I read the tag block and stop when I hit the anchor block's PoE and ±10 cm. I scroll fast past screen 6 and would only slow down on "not people", where "and yours" catches my eye and leaves me uneasy. Then the footer for my link.

## 4. The question I still have

Who gets told first when a trolley waits too long, me or my manager? The page still never says. "Your supervisor can see it during the shift" is the nearest it gets, and "can see" is not an alert. Second: does AmIHub open on my phone or not? Third: what happens when the tag falls off?

## 5. Score: 8/10

The walk is still seen. The "trolley, not the person" line is now on screen 2, in my words. "Watches" is gone. "Last seen" and "how close depends on your floor" are honest. It loses points because the phone is still a maybe, nobody says who gets the alert first, and "Tagging people is a separate decision, and yours" puts the fear back in on the last screen.

The line I would read aloud to my crew: "It goes on the trolley, not on the person pushing it."

## 6. Compared with v2 (7/10)

Better. Both fixes I asked for landed: the not-people line moved to screen 2 in floor words, and "watches" became "follows".
Not a 10 because the phone is still unconfirmed, the alert owner is still unsaid, and the new "and yours" line hands my crew a reason to worry that v2 did not have.

## 7. Best and worst lines

- **Best:** "It goes on the trolley, not on the person pushing it." (Screen 2.) It beats "not a walk" now, because it answers the first thing my crew will ask.
- **Worst:** "Tagging people is a separate decision, and yours." (Screen 6.) To a plant head it says "your choice". To my crew it says "people tagging is on the menu". Runner-up, for reading load: "Before it starts, we agree with you how each wait counts, even one still open at shift end." (18 words.)

## 8. One change that would make it a 10

Answer the phone and the alert in one plain line on screen 2, under the capture, and show it on a real phone at phone size: "On your phone. The alert comes to the person at the line first." If either is not true today, say so plainly. And cut "and yours" from screen 6, or move the people-tagging choice off the homepage to People and privacy, where the plant head and EHS go to read it.

Still open for the founders: who gets the wait alert first. If it reaches my manager before me, I am against it from day one.
