I read in two voices: **Procurement** ("what am I buying, what recurs, who supports it") and **Promoter** ("who are these people, will they come when it breaks").

Read: section 3 copy and picture descriptions, section 4 nav and footer. Nothing else.

## 3. Where I would have stopped

Procurement does not stop. Screens 1, 5 and 6 answer most of what I came for, and "How to buy" gives me a next page. The promoter reads further than in v2: "local engineers on site" and "before you decide on more" carry him through screen 6. He stops at "Book a call". He still has not seen one person. He would not book it himself. He would forward it to me with "find out who these people are, and ask if a founder comes".

## 4. The question I still have

Procurement: "Is installation and support in the pilot price, and which lines recur every year after (AmIHub, AMC, batteries)?" Promoter: "Who are the founders, and will one of them be on the call?"

## 5. Score

**8 / 10.** Procurement can name what is bought, how price is shared, who supports it and the buying steps within 90 seconds. The promoter now has a reason to trust the support ("local engineers on site") but still no face, and no other user signal.

## 6. Compared with v2 for me (7)

**Better.** The price line, "How to buy" back in the nav and footer, the fixed "final figure", and "before you decide on more" close three of my v2 flags. The face, a true stop line, and named installation, support and recurring costs are still missing, so it is one point, not three.

## 7. Best and worst line

**Best:** "Designed and built in India, with local stock and local engineers on site." (screen 6). It turns "you're a start-up" into "they will come when it breaks", in the voice the promoter trusts.

**Worst:** "The first waits it times are today's, so you can use them as your baseline." (screen 6). The only line I read twice, in the block a pilot approver reads most closely. Suggest: "The pilot times today's waits first, so you have your baseline."

Finish the "What you buy" block and put a person under the call, in one place:

> **What you buy.** AmIGo tags, AmIMo anchors and AmIHub, plus our survey, installation and support on your floor. [Which parts recur each year, named, no figures.] Designed and built in India, with local stock and local engineers on site. One team owns every layer, so you have one contract and one support line. Prices and pilot options are shared under NDA, after the first call.
>
> On the call: [founder name], who built it. [photo; public details or written approval only]

Procurement gets deployment as its own part and the recurring lines. The promoter gets a face and asks to meet them. Both depend on founder answers (items 11 and 12).

## Flags for the Buyer Truth Lead and the Truth and Quality Lead

- **No face.** Pass test still fails for the promoter. Item 12. Bios AMBER: public details or written approval only.
- **Installation and support not named** as parts of what is bought. Only the survey is. Procurement cannot tell if installation is a separate line.
- **Recurring costs unnamed.** Battery life is stated; who replaces batteries, and whether AmIHub or an AMC recurs, is not.
- **No stop line.** "Before you decide on more" helps, but the cautious approver still has no stated exit. Item 1.
- **Anchor count set after survey.** Honest. /how-to-buy/ and /tools/anchors/ must say the quote follows the survey, so no one reads the estimate as a quote.
- **"Time spent searching" tool.** Confirm no saving, payback or ROI figure (NEVER in file 03). I want to see its output before launch.
- **"Your MES stays in charge of every job."** Commercial obligation. Confirm with founders before it ships.
- **"One AmIHub console can also run more than one site."** AMBER, item 10. If it does not ship today, the promoter will ask on the call and the trust is lost there.
- **No "who else uses it" signal.** Deployment line still cut (STOP). A gap, not an error.
- **No RED found.** No funding, revenue, traction count, investor name, customer name, logo, testimonial, ROI or exact pilot price or duration.
