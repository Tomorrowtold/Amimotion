### Three rounds of rework on v3

1. **Truth first (Truth and Quality Lead).** Each contradiction a reader found was settled by the source, not by taste. We wrote down the true chain once (pulse, anchors, AmIHub, last seen, job link, MES) and checked every screen against it. Two v2 lines that sat on binding REWORDs were wrong or loose (A16's "reports its own position", A43's "no data cables"). The new wording is stricter. Three answers v2 had marked as founder-blocked were in fact in the brochure: prices under NDA, local engineers on site, and multi-site from one console. They are now on the page.
2. **Words (Head of Words).** Every twice-read line was rewritten and read once by a fresh reader on the committee. "Carriers" is now trolleys, bins and pallets on every screen a supervisor reads. "Watches" is now "follows". "Final figure" is gone. Longest sentence on the page is 18 words. Average is under 10 words. Commas are under one per sentence on every screen.
3. **Length (Creative Director and Experience Design Lead).** Each fix added words. We then cut every line that no reader would miss. "Running more than one plant? Ask us how the second one starts." became one shorter true line. The call block went from 18 words to 11. The protocol line lost "Built on" from its own block and kept it as one short line. On screens 5 and 6 each reader still reads the headline and one block. The 90 seconds still hold (section 3).
