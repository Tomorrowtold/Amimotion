### The shared world (for every floor picture)

One model hall, built once in Blender and rendered at 8K so every still is a crop of one master. Camera A is locked: three-quarter overhead, about 45 degrees down, about 11 m up, near 35 mm equivalent.

The art direction is the truth check's section 0, word for word:

> The hall is a designed model, never a photograph. One flat matte material per kind of thing. Simplified shapes. No wear, grime, tyre marks, patched slabs or faded paint. No daylight realism, sun patches or depth of field on wide shots. No people. No signs, text, brands or numbers. Blue trails and amber tints are flat graphic marks. Nothing in it suggests a real or named site.

What is in it. Steel portal columns every 6 m. Four AmIMo anchors on columns at about 5 m, from the CAD geometry, in the world's flat dark grey. Top left a press line with two presses. Middle two assembly stations. Right of centre an inspection station with a queue zone marked by a pale grey floor outline. Pallet racks. On the far right a staging lane and two closed dock doors. Six mesh trolleys with bins. One fixture holding a sub-assembly. One tool cart. One tugger pulling three trolleys. One forklift with a pallet. One AMR carrying a bin. Factory and stores under one roof, for P8.

Light. Even, soft, shadowless ambient light with soft contact shadows only.

Colour script. Floor #f6f4ef. Machines #e4e2dc. Shadow #c9c7c1. Hardware #3a3d42. Located and moving: blue #66a5fa. Waiting: amber #f0a43a. Nothing else.

Marks. Trails are thin flat lines of equal weight, one per tagged thing, with no thickening where flow is heavy (A29). Amber is a flat, crisp-edged tint on the floor under a waiting thing. No glow, rings, pulses, chips, numbers or arrows.

Fixed counts. Wherever waits show, three trolleys wait in the inspection queue and one pallet waits in the staging lane. No carrier leaves the queue ahead of one that has waited longer.

Higgsfield credits: zero.
