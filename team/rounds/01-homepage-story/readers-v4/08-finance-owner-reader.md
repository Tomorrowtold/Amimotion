# Reader 08: Finance and Owner (P9 procurement, P11 promoter). Round 1, homepage v4

SIMULATION. I am not a real buyer. I am a chartered accountant who has sat beside a promoter for nine years. I am reading on a phone between a vendor registration and a payment-term dispute. I read in two voices: **Procurement** ("what am I buying, what recurs, who supports it") and **Promoter** ("who are these people, will they come when it breaks").

Read: section 3 copy and picture descriptions, section 4 nav and footer. Nothing else.

---

## 1. After the hero only

"AmIMotion sells a small tag for each trolley, bin and tool, anchors that go on my columns, and one live map that shows where each thing went and how long it has waited."

Right. The hero has not changed and it did not need to. I can still write three BOQ headings from it. Promoter: still a clean model hall, nothing Indian, nobody's face. He waits, as before. The hero is not where he decides.

## 3. Where I would have stopped

Procurement does not stop. Screens 1, 5 and 6 carry me, and "How to buy" is the next page. The promoter, for the first time, does not stop short of the call. In v3 he stopped at "Book a call" because nobody was behind it. Now two names are behind it, and the founder leads it. He reads to the button. The only drag is the shift-end sentence in the pilot block, which slows me, not him.

## 4. The question I still have

Procurement: "Which lines repeat every year after the pilot (AmIHub licence, AMC, batteries), and is installation and the AmIHub PC in the price or on us?" Promoter: "Who else runs this? I'll ask Kaushal on the call."

**9 / 10.** Procurement can name the three parts, two services, the price route, one contract and one support line, and the buying steps inside 90 seconds. The promoter can now name the people and why to trust them: made in India, local engineers on his site, and the founder on the call. I would forward it today, to the promoter, with "book this". One point off for installation and recurring lines still unnamed, and two lines read twice.

## 6. Compared with v3 for me (8)

**Better.** The founders are named on the call, the baseline line reads once, and battery level, phone setup, dark-anchor alerts and "without re-fitting the first bay" answer support and write-off questions I had. Installation, the AmIHub PC and the recurring lines are still not named, and one new re-read appeared in the pilot block.

## 7. Best and worst line

**Best:** "The call is a live demo on real AmIHub data, not slides. Led by Kaushal Kukkar, founder and CEO, and Vivek Dham, CTO." (screen 6). Real data, and the founder in the room. That is the ten-minute decision I have watched promoters make.

**Worst:** "Before it starts, we agree with you how a wait still open at shift end counts." (screen 6). A re-read in the block a pilot approver reads most closely. Suggest: "Before it starts, we agree how to count a wait that runs past shift end."

## 8. One change that would make it a 10

Name installation and the recurring lines in "What you buy", with no figures:

> **What you buy.** AmIGo tags, AmIMo anchors and AmIHub, plus our survey, an anchor plan and installation on your floor. After that, [name what recurs: AmIHub licence or none, support or AMC, batteries]. Designed and built in India...

Procurement then has every line of the BOQ named before the NDA, and nothing reads as hidden scope. Depends on founder answers on installation scope, the AmIHub PC and what recurs.

## Flags for the Buyer Truth Lead and the Truth and Quality Lead

- **Installation not named** as a part of what is bought. Survey and anchor plan are. Procurement cannot tell if installation is a separate line.
- **Recurring costs unnamed.** Battery level is shown; who replaces batteries, and whether AmIHub or an AMC recurs, is not.
- **AmIHub PC.** "Runs on a PC inside your plant." Supplied or customer-bought? Not in "What you buy". Hidden-scope risk.
- **Job linking scope.** "Link each trolley to its job once" Who does it, and is it in the pilot? /how-to-buy/ or /pilot/ should say.
- **Founder names.** "Led by Kaushal Kukkar, founder and CEO, and Vivek Dham, CTO." AMBER, public details only, as ruled. If either cannot be on every first call, the promoter's trust breaks on the call. Confirm with founders that the line is a promise they keep.
- **No stop line.** "Before you decide on more" carries it, but no stated exit. B46 STOP holds; noted as a gap, not an error.
- **"A second plant joins the same console"** AMBER, indicative. If multi-site is not shipping, the promoter will ask on the call.
- **"Time spent searching" tool.** Confirm no saving, payback or ROI figure (NEVER in file 03).
- **No "who else uses it" signal.** Deployment line still cut (STOP). A gap, not an error.
- **No RED found.** No funding, revenue, traction count, investor name, customer name, logo, testimonial, ROI, or exact pilot price, duration or deployment day.
