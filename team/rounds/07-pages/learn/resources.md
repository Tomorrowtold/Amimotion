## Head

**The short answer:**

AmIMotion's resources help a plant plan real-time location before the first call: a six-question floor diagnostic, an anchor estimator, a search-time calculator, a one-page pilot planner, fourteen field guides, an FAQ and a glossary. None of them asks for an email. Each figure carries its condition, such as positions down to ±10 cm in 3D with AmIMo anchors, depending on the site.

Picture: none.

## Tools that work it out. (lede: Each one runs in your browser. Nothing is stored and nobody asks for an email.) tiles, cols=2

- **[Plan your first bay](/diagnostic/)** Six questions about how work moves on your floor. AmIMotion's diagnostic sketches your floor as you answer and ends with a visibility profile and a pilot scope for one bay or line.
- **[How many anchors you need](/tools/anchors/)** Your floor size and racking in, a first anchor range out. AmIMotion's estimator shows its planning grid, and a site survey sets the real number.
- **[Time spent searching](/tools/search-time/)** Your people, searches and minutes, multiplied into hours a year. AmIMotion's calculator assumes no savings.
- **[Your pilot on one page](/tools/pilot/)** Three choices become a one-page brief for an AmIMotion pilot, to share with your team or bring to the first call.

## Pages that explain it. tiles, cols=3

- **[Field guides](/learn/)** Fourteen guides, one question each: UWB vs RFID, anchor counts, accuracy, dwell time, job links and choosing an RTLS in India.
- **[Questions](/faq/)** Short answers about AmIMotion: the company, the three parts, accuracy, cabling, data, standards and AI assistants.
- **[Glossary](/glossary/)** 39 terms you hear when people talk about location on a factory floor, from RTLS and UWB to dwell and last seen.

## Where to begin. (steps)

1. **Name the flow** Pick the flow where people walk to find work. AmIMotion's diagnostic helps you choose it.
2. **Size the floor** Get a first anchor range from the estimator, before anyone visits.
3. **Count the cost** Put your own search time into hours a year, as the start of a baseline.
4. **Bring a brief** Turn it into a one-page AmIMotion pilot brief for the first call.
